## Description

Plan: .track/docs/sales-voice-agent-demo-target-plan.md §W3 T-0188 (lines 252-274). Review: §4 (rolling deploys). Tier 1.

## Goal
Schedule and automatically initiate the call without a dashboard click.

## Scope
- `scheduled_calls` table: `scheduled_at`, `status`, `session_id`, attempt count, lease/claim fields, provider call ID.
- Scheduler plugin (kind `trigger` or new `scheduler`) with `schedule`, `claim_due`, `complete`, `fail` operations, registered through the existing plugin registry so it shows in the Plugins tab.
- Single-task poller in the voice service; on restart it resumes due jobs from libSQL.
- Ordering rule: claim with lease → mark `starting` → invoke the Twilio seam → persist call SID. A job stuck in `starting` past its lease is surfaced as `failed` for operator action, never auto-retried. "No duplicate calls" wins over "always call".
- Telemetry for schedule, claim, provider start, failure.
- Extend `DemoApp.reset()` to clear `scheduled_calls`.
- EventBridge replacement stays out of scope.

## Acceptance
- Fake-clock test: schedule → advance time → claimed once → Twilio seam invoked once.
- Two concurrent pollers (models an ECS rolling deploy, `infra/demo.ts` scaling 1/1) claim the same job exactly once.
- Restart during `starting` does not place a second call; the session shows `failed` with reason.
- A failed provider call is visible in session state.

## Write scope
Scheduler plugin/service, schema, worker tests, `packages/demo/src/server.ts` (poller mount only). Serialize with T-0186/T-0189.
