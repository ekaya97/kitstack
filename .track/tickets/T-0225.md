## Goal

Make storage and connector implementations reusable plugins behind scoped,
secret-safe host bindings.

## Scope

- Enforce org/kit scope metadata at the provider-neutral boundary.
- Keep secret references separate from manifests, kit context, and telemetry.
- Validate connector capabilities and required scopes at binding time.
- Add contract coverage for SQL/object storage and REST/OpenAPI binding.

## Non-goals

No universal connector catalog, CRM/calendar implementation, marketplace, or
provider-specific business behavior.

## Acceptance

- scope and secret separation are testable;
- connector binding failures are deterministic and non-sensitive;
- existing SDK storage/connectors tests/build pass;
- no plugin-registry or debrief files change.
