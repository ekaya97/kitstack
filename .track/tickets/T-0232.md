## Goal

Add one narrow adapter to a selected connector infrastructure service without
making that service KitStack's runtime.

## Scope

After pilot selection, evaluate Nango first and Pipedream Connect for long-tail
coverage. Wrap only OAuth/token lifecycle, provider invocation, webhook/sync
cursor, normalized errors, and metadata propagation required by the existing
KitStack Connector contract. Keep provider tokens behind SecretReference.

## Acceptance

The external adapter passes the local contract suite and can be removed or
replaced without changing a kit. No universal connector catalog or agent
execution model is introduced.
