# SEO Essentials for Blog Posts

This document covers on-page SEO best practices for blog content. Use it to ensure every blog post has proper SEO signals without sacrificing readability.

---

## Keyword Strategy

### Primary Keyword
Every post should target one primary keyword or phrase. This is the term you want the post to rank for in search results.

**How to identify the right keyword:**
- It should match the search intent of the post (informational, commercial, transactional)
- It should have search volume (people actually search for this)
- It should be achievable given the site's authority (don't target "best CRM" on a new blog)
- It should be specific enough to be useful ("freelance pricing strategies" not "pricing")

**If the user doesn't provide a keyword:**
- Infer the most likely search query based on the topic
- Suggest 2-3 keyword options with reasoning
- Default to the most specific, long-tail option (easier to rank, more targeted traffic)

### Keyword Placement Rules

| Location | Priority | How to Apply |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Title (H1) | Critical | Include naturally, front-load when possible |
| First 100 words | Critical | Work into the introduction naturally |
| At least one H2 | High | Use in the most relevant section header |
| Meta description | High | Include once, naturally |
| URL slug | High | Use exact keyword or close variation |
| Image alt text | Medium | Use in the most relevant image description |
| Body text | Medium | 0.5-1.5% density across the full post |
| Last 100 words | Low | Not required, but helps if natural |

### Keyword Density Guidelines

For a 1,000-word post targeting "client onboarding checklist":

| Density | Occurrences | Assessment |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| 0.3% | 3 | Too thin — may not rank |
| 0.5% | 5 | Minimum — OK for low-competition keywords |
| 1.0% | 10 | Ideal — good signal without stuffing |
| 1.5% | 15 | Upper limit — only if every use is natural |
| 2.0%+ | 20+ | Too dense — Google may flag as keyword stuffing |

**Count semantic variations as half-credit:** If the keyword is "client onboarding checklist," then "onboarding process," "new client checklist," and "client onboarding plan" all support the primary keyword without repeating it.

## Title Optimization

### Effective Blog Title Formulas

| Formula | Example | When to Use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| How to [Achieve Result] | "How to Write a Client Proposal That Wins" | Tutorials, guides |
| [Number] [Noun] to [Benefit] | "7 Pricing Mistakes That Cost Freelancers Thousands" | Listicles |
| [Keyword]: [Expanded Explanation] | "Value-Based Pricing: A Complete Guide for Consultants" | Deep dives |
| [X] vs. [Y]: [Question] | "Hourly vs. Project Pricing: Which Earns You More?" | Comparisons |
| Why [Surprising Thing] | "Why Charging More Actually Gets You More Clients" | Opinion pieces |
| The [Adjective] Guide to [Topic] | "The Practical Guide to Client Onboarding" | Comprehensive guides |

### Title Rules
1. **50-65 characters** for full display in Google search results
2. **Front-load the keyword** when possible (first 50% of the title)
3. **Promise specific value** — the reader should know what they'll get
4. **Avoid clickbait** — deliver on what the title promises
5. **Use power words sparingly:** "proven," "complete," "essential" — one per title max
6. **Test readability:** Would you click this title? Does it stand out in a feed?

## Meta Description

### Purpose
The meta description appears below the title in search results. It doesn't directly affect ranking but dramatically affects click-through rate.

### Formula
```
[What the post covers] + [benefit to the reader] + [implicit or explicit CTA]
```

### Examples

**Good:**
> "Learn the 5-phase client onboarding process used by top agencies. Includes checklists, email templates, and real examples. Start onboarding better today."

**Bad:**
> "This article discusses client onboarding. We will cover the phases of onboarding and provide some tips. Read more on our blog."

### Rules
1. **150-160 characters** (Google truncates beyond this)
2. **Include the primary keyword** once, naturally
3. **Write it as a mini-pitch** — why should someone click?
4. **Don't duplicate the title** — the meta description should expand on the title, not repeat it
5. **Use active voice** — "Learn how to..." not "This post discusses..."

## Header Structure (H2, H3)

### Why Headers Matter for SEO
- Google uses headers to understand content structure and relevance
- Readers scan headers to decide if the article is worth reading
- Headers create anchor points for featured snippets and "People Also Ask" boxes

### Header Hierarchy

```
# H1 — Article title (only one per page)
  ## H2 — Major sections (3-8 per article)
    ### H3 — Subsections within an H2 (0-4 per H2)
      #### H4 — Rarely needed, only for complex deep dives
```

### Header Rules
1. **One keyword in one H2** — don't put the keyword in every H2
2. **Headers should be scannable** — a reader who only reads H2s should understand the article
3. **Use question-form headers** when appropriate — these can trigger featured snippets
4. **Keep headers under 60 characters** when possible
5. **No header should be followed by another header** — there must be body text between headers
6. **Headers are not sentences** — no periods at the end

## Internal Linking

### Why It Matters
Internal links help Google discover and understand your site structure. They also keep readers on your site longer and distribute page authority across your content.

### Rules
1. **2-4 internal links per 1,000-word post**
2. **Link to relevant content** — not random pages. A post about pricing should link to a pricing page, a proposal post, or a related blog post.
3. **Use descriptive anchor text** — "our guide to project pricing" not "click here" or "read more"
4. **Link naturally** — the sentence should work with or without the link
5. **Vary anchor text** — don't use the same anchor text for the same page across multiple posts
6. **Link early** — place the most important internal link within the first 300 words

### Where to Link
- Related blog posts on the same topic
- Service pages related to the topic
- Product pages if the post is commercial
- Pillar content that this post supports

---

## External Linking

### Rules
1. **1-2 external links per post** — to authoritative sources
2. **Never link to competitors** — link to research, statistics, or tools instead
3. **Use for credibility** — back up claims with external sources
4. **Open in new tab** — external links should not navigate away from your site (add `target="_blank"`)
5. **Don't use nofollow on legitimate sources** — follow links to authoritative sites is a positive signal

## URL Slug

### Rules
1. **Short and descriptive:** `/blog/client-onboarding-checklist` not `/blog/the-complete-guide-to-client-onboarding-in-2024`
2. **Include the primary keyword** when possible
3. **Use hyphens** to separate words (not underscores)
4. **Lowercase only**
5. **No dates in URLs** — content should be evergreen; updating the date shouldn't require a new URL
6. **No stop words** unless they're part of the keyword ("how-to" is fine; "the-best-way-to" is too long)

## Featured Snippet Optimization

### What Triggers Featured Snippets
Google pulls content into featured snippets (position 0) when it directly answers a search query. Three formats trigger snippets:

**Paragraph snippets:**
- Answer a question in 40-60 words immediately after a question-form H2
- "What is value-based pricing? Value-based pricing is a model where you price your services based on the business outcome they create, rather than the time they take..."

**List snippets:**
- Use ordered or unordered lists after a "How to..." or "Steps to..." header
- Google pulls the list directly into the snippet

**Table snippets:**
- Use tables to compare data
- Google pulls well-structured tables into snippets

### How to Optimize
1. Include one question-form H2 per post (e.g., "What is [topic]?" or "How does [topic] work?")
2. Answer the question directly in the paragraph immediately after the H2
3. Keep the answer to 40-60 words — concise and complete
4. Follow with expanded detail for readers who want more

## Image SEO

When suggesting images, always include:

| Element | What to Include |
|---------|----------------|
| Alt text | Descriptive text with keyword if relevant: "Client onboarding checklist showing 5 phases from welcome to check-in" |
| File name suggestion | `client-onboarding-checklist-phases.png` (not `image1.png`) |
| Caption (optional) | A sentence that adds context not already in the body text |
| Placement | After the paragraph that introduces the concept the image illustrates |

### Image Suggestions to Include
- One featured image (top of post or after introduction)
- One image per 300-400 words for longer posts
- Types: diagrams, screenshots, comparison tables as images, charts, infographics
- If the user doesn't have images, suggest what they should create or use as stock
