# Blog Post Structure Template

Use this skeleton for every blog post. Fill in the bracketed sections based on the user's input, selected structure pattern, and tone preferences.

---

```markdown
# [Title — 50-65 characters, keyword front-loaded]

*Meta description: [150-160 characters — value proposition + keyword + reason to click]*
*Target keyword: [primary keyword]*
*Suggested URL: /blog/[keyword-slug]*

---

## [Introduction — 100-150 words]

[Hook: open with ONE of these approaches]
- A surprising statistic or data point
- A relatable problem the reader experiences
- A bold or contrarian statement
- A direct question

[Context: 1-2 sentences establishing the problem or opportunity]

[Promise: what the reader will learn, gain, or be able to do after reading]

[Transition into the body — no "let's dive in" or "without further ado"]

---

## [H2 — Section 1: Most Important Point]

[2-4 paragraphs covering this section's topic]

[Include at least one of:]
- A concrete example
- A data point or statistic
- A practical tip or actionable advice
- A table or comparison

[Suggested image: describe what visual would enhance this section]

---

## [H2 — Section 2]

[2-4 paragraphs]

[Supporting content: examples, data, or actionable details]

---

## [H2 — Section 3]

[2-4 paragraphs]

[Supporting content]

---

## [H2 — Section 4 (if needed)]

[2-4 paragraphs]

---

## [H2 — Additional Sections as Needed]

[Scale the number of H2 sections to the content type:]
- How-to: one H2 per step (5-10 sections)
- Listicle: one H2 per item (5-15 sections)
- Deep-dive: one H2 per major topic (4-8 sections)
- Comparison: one H2 per criterion + overview sections (5-8 sections)
- Opinion: 3-5 sections

---

## [Conclusion — 75-100 words]

[Key takeaway: one sentence summarizing the main point]

[Reinforcement: why this matters for the reader]

[NO new information — summarize, don't introduce]

[Natural transition to CTA]

---

**[Call to Action]**

[One specific, clear action the reader should take]
[Phrased as a benefit to the reader, not a request from you]
[Connected to the topic of the post]

Examples:
- "Download our free [resource] to [benefit]."
- "Try [tool/service] free for 14 days."
- "Book a free [consultation/audit] to see how this applies to your business."
- "Subscribe for weekly [topic] insights delivered to your inbox."
```

---

## Pre-Writing Checklist

Before writing, confirm you have:

- [ ] Topic or outline from the user
- [ ] Target audience identified
- [ ] Goal of the post defined (inform, persuade, convert)
- [ ] Target keyword (or inferred keyword)
- [ ] Word count target (or default: 800-1,200)
- [ ] Tone setting (or default: conversational-professional)
- [ ] CTA defined (or default: relevant to the topic)
- [ ] Structure pattern selected (how-to, listicle, deep-dive, comparison, opinion)

## Post-Writing Checklist

After writing, verify:

- [ ] Title is 50-65 characters with keyword
- [ ] Meta description is 150-160 characters
- [ ] Keyword appears in first 100 words
- [ ] Keyword appears in at least one H2
- [ ] Keyword density is 0.5-1.5%
- [ ] No paragraph exceeds 4 sentences
- [ ] No sentence exceeds 30 words
- [ ] Introduction hooks within the first 2 sentences
- [ ] Every H2 is scannable and self-explanatory
- [ ] At least 2 internal link suggestions included
- [ ] CTA is clear and specific
- [ ] Word count is within ±10% of target
- [ ] No placeholder text remains
- [ ] No filler paragraphs (every paragraph adds information)
- [ ] Conclusion does not introduce new information

## Formatting Quick Reference

| Element | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|
| **Bold** | Key terms, important figures, recommendations (max 10% of text) |
| *Italic* | Definitions, titles of works, emphasis (sparingly) |
| Bullet lists | Collections of equal-weight items (3-7 items) |
| Numbered lists | Sequential steps or ranked items |
| Tables | Comparisons across multiple dimensions |
| Blockquotes | Expert quotes, important callouts, data highlights |
| H2 | Major sections (3-10 per post) |
| H3 | Subsections within an H2 (0-4 per H2) |
| Horizontal rule | Between major thematic shifts (use sparingly) |

## Word Count Guidelines by Type

| Post Type | Target | Min | Max |
|-----------|--------|-----|-----|
| How-to (simple) | 800 | 600 | 1,200 |
| How-to (complex) | 1,500 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| Listicle | 1,000 | 700 | 1,500 |
| Deep-dive | 2,000 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Comparison | 1,200 | 900 | 2,000 |
| Opinion | 800 | 600 | 1,200 |
| News/update | 500 | 300 | 800 |
