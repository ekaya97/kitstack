## Usage Notes for the Template

- **Voice Summary** must be specific enough to differentiate this brand from every other brand. Test: read the summary without the brand name — could it describe someone else? If yes, rewrite.
- **Dimension Scores** always include the "What This Means in Practice" column. A score without interpretation is useless.
- **Tone Rules** are prescriptive, not descriptive. "We are friendly" is descriptive. "Address the reader as 'you' in every piece" is prescriptive.
- **Vocabulary lists** are the most referenced section. Keep them tight and alphabetical if the list exceeds 15 words.
- **Example Rewrites** prove the guide works. If you cannot rewrite a generic paragraph and make it sound distinctly like this brand, the guide is not specific enough.
- **Quick Reference Card** must fit in a Slack message. If it is longer than 15 lines, cut it down.
- **Channel Adaptations** are optional for solo founders. Include them for any brand with more than one writer or more than two communication channels.
