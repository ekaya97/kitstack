## The Challenge

Kiez Immobilien was growing fast. With 35 agents across Berlin's competitive real estate market, they were handling hundreds of leads per month — and managing them in spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

The system worked when they had 10 agents. At 35, it was breaking down. Leads were assigned by forwarding messages in a group chat. Follow-ups were tracked in personal spreadsheets, if they were tracked at all. When an agent was sick or on vacation, their leads sat untouched. There was no visibility into who was handling what, no automated reminders, and no way to match incoming leads with available properties in real time.

The cost was measurable: roughly 30% of new leads were falling through the cracks. They'd arrive, get lost in a thread, and never receive a follow-up. For a real estate agency where a single closed deal can be worth €10,000-€30,000 in commission, every lost lead was a direct hit to revenue.

"We knew we were leaving money on the table," said Thomas Richter, founder of Kiez Immobilien. "But we didn't know how much until we actually tracked it. It was painful."

## The Approach

We started with a two-week discovery phase — not designing software, but shadowing the team. We sat with agents, watched how they processed leads, and mapped every step from initial inquiry to closed deal. The goal was to build a CRM that matched their actual workflow, not force them into a generic one.

### Phase 1: Discovery & Architecture (Weeks 1-3)

We documented 14 distinct touchpoints in their lead-to-close pipeline and identified three critical failure points:

1. **Lead intake:** New leads arrived through 6 different channels (website, portals, referrals, walk-ins, phone, social media) with no centralized capture.
2. **Assignment:** Lead assignment was manual and inconsistent. High-performing agents got more leads, lower-performing agents got frustrated and left.
3. **Follow-up:** There was no system for follow-up reminders. If an agent didn't respond within the first hour, the probability of conversion dropped by 50%.

### Phase 2: Core CRM Build (Weeks 4-10)

We built a custom CRM with four core modules:

- **Centralized lead inbox:** All channels feed into a single dashboard. Every lead gets a timestamp, source tag, and automatic assignment based on agent availability, location, and specialization.
- **Automated follow-up sequences:** When a lead comes in, the system triggers an immediate acknowledgment email. If no agent responds within 30 minutes, it escalates. At 2 hours, it reassigns.
- **Property matching engine:** Based on the lead's criteria (budget, location, property type), the system surfaces matching listings automatically. Agents send curated options with one click instead of manually searching.
- **Pipeline visibility:** A real-time dashboard showing every lead's status, every agent's capacity, and conversion rates by source, agent, and property type.

### Phase 3: Mobile App (Weeks 11-14)

Berlin real estate agents spend most of their day showing properties, not sitting at desks. We built a mobile companion app that gives agents full CRM access from their phones: incoming leads, scheduled viewings, follow-up reminders, and the ability to log notes after a showing in 30 seconds.

### Phase 4: Migration & Training (Weeks 15-16)

We migrated 18 months of historical lead data from their spreadsheets, ran a half-day training session with all 35 agents, and provided two weeks of on-site support during the transition.

## The Results

Six months after launch, the impact was clear across every metric that matters in real estate.

| Deals closed per month | ~22 | ~31 | +40% |
| Average lead response time | 14 hours | 23 minutes | -97% |
| Leads lost (no follow-up) | ~30% | <3% | -90% |
| Agent adoption rate | N/A | 94% daily active | — |

The 40% increase in closed deals, at Kiez Immobilien's average commission, translates to approximately €180,000-€270,000 in additional annual commission revenue.

The response time improvement alone was transformative. In Berlin's real estate market, where buyers often inquire about multiple properties simultaneously, the first agent to respond has a significant advantage. Moving from 14 hours to 23 minutes meant Kiez Immobilien was consistently first to respond — a competitive edge that compounds over time.

Perhaps most importantly, the near-elimination of lost leads restored agent confidence. The team knew that every lead was being tracked, followed up on, and assigned fairly. Agent turnover, which had been a growing concern, stabilized.
