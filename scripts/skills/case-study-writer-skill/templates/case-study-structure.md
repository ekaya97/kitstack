# Case Study Structure Template

Use this skeleton for every case study. Select the framework (CSR, STAR, or Before/After) and fill in the bracketed sections based on the user's input.

---

## Pre-Writing Checklist

Before drafting, confirm you have:

- [ ] Client name (or anonymization decision)
- [ ] Industry and company size
- [ ] The problem/challenge they faced
- [ ] What you specifically did (services, methodology, timeline)
- [ ] Quantified results (3-5 metrics minimum)
- [ ] Client quote (real or to be drafted for approval)
- [ ] Client approval status
- [ ] Target audience for the case study

---

## Headline Formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| How [Client] [Achieved Result] | "How Kiez Immobilien Increased Deal Closings by 40%" |
| [Metric] [Result]: [Client] Story | "74% Error Reduction: The Rhein Logistik Warehouse Transformation" |
| From [Bad State] to [Good State] | "From Drugstore Aesthetic to Premium Shelf: A DTC Skincare Rebrand" |
| [Client] [Action] in [Timeframe] | "How a B2B SaaS Company Tripled Demo Bookings in 90 Days" |
| The [Amount] [Saving/Gain]: [Brief Description] | "The €280,000 Annual Saving: Redesigning Warehouse Operations" |

**Rules:**
- Always include a specific metric in the headline
- Lead with the result, not the client name
- 8-15 words
- Active voice

## Post-Writing Checklist

After drafting, verify:

- [ ] Headline includes a specific metric
- [ ] Client snapshot is complete (industry, size, services, timeline, key result)
- [ ] Challenge section makes the reader empathize (they see themselves in the problem)
- [ ] Solution section demonstrates expertise without oversharing methodology
- [ ] Results section has 3-5 specific, quantified metrics with before/after
- [ ] Every metric has context (timeframe, comparison, business significance)
- [ ] Client quote is present and adds a human voice
- [ ] CTA is clear and connected to the topic
- [ ] Word count is appropriate (500-800 standard, 800-1,200 complex)
- [ ] No placeholder text remains
- [ ] Anonymization is applied correctly (if needed)
- [ ] The client is positioned as the hero, not you
- [ ] No exaggerated claims or unsupported superlatives

## Length Guidelines

| Case Study Type | Word Count | When |
|----------------|------------|------|
| Portfolio card / teaser | 50-100 words | Website grid, quick overview |
| LinkedIn post | 200-300 words | Social sharing |
| Standard case study | 500-800 words | Most marketing use cases |
| Detailed case study | 800-1,200 words | Complex engagements, sales collateral |
| Long-form / white paper | 1,200-2,000 words | Awards, thought leadership |

Most case studies should land at 600-900 words. If you're exceeding 1,200 words, you're likely including too much process detail. Cut the methodology section first — readers care most about the challenge and the results.
