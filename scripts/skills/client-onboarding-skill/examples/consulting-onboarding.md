# Example: Consulting Engagement Onboarding

## User Input

> I'm a solo management consultant and I just landed a new client — a mid-size logistics company (about 200 employees) in Hamburg. They hired me to help with their digital transformation strategy. The engagement is 3 months, €45,000 total, paid monthly. I need to interview their leadership team, assess current processes, and deliver a transformation roadmap. I use Asana for PM and mostly communicate via email.

---

## Generated Output

# Client Onboarding Checklist
**Client:** Mid-Size Logistics Company (Hamburg, ~200 employees)
**Project:** Digital Transformation Strategy & Roadmap
**Budget:** €45,000 (€15,000/month x 3) | **Timeline:** 12 weeks
**Your Role:** Solo management consultant

---

## Phase 1: Welcome (Day 0-1)

**Goal:** Establish credibility, collect context, and lock in the kickoff.

- [ ] **Send welcome email within 2 hours of contract signing** (you)
  - Reference the digital transformation initiative specifically
  - Outline the 12-week journey at a high level: Discovery (weeks 1-4) → Analysis (weeks 5-8) → Roadmap (weeks 9-12)
  - Attach the intake questionnaire
  - Propose 2-3 kickoff meeting slots
  - Provide your direct contact details
- [ ] **Send executive intake questionnaire** (you) — include:
  - What triggered the decision to pursue digital transformation now?
  - What does "digital transformation" mean to your leadership team? (This question surfaces misaligned expectations)
  - What are your top 3 business pain points today?
  - Which departments or processes are most affected?
  - Have you attempted any digital initiatives before? What happened?
  - Who are the key stakeholders I should interview? (Request name, title, and role in the initiative)
  - Are there any internal politics or sensitivities I should be aware of?
  - Do you have existing documentation? (process maps, org chart, IT architecture, previous consultant reports)
  - What does success look like at the end of 12 weeks?
  - Budget beyond consulting: is there implementation budget allocated?
- [ ] **Request documentation package** (you)
  - Organizational chart
  - Current IT systems inventory
  - Recent strategic plans or board presentations
  - Any previous digital/IT audit reports
  - Financial overview (revenue, cost structure — enough to understand scale)
- [ ] **Send NDA for execution** (you) — logistics companies handle sensitive operational and client data
- [ ] **Send first invoice — €15,000 (Month 1)** (you)
- [ ] **Schedule kickoff for Day 3-5** (you)

---

## Phase 2: Setup (Day 1-3)

**Goal:** Prepare a sharp kickoff by studying the company and planning the interview process.

- [ ] **Create Asana project** (you)
  - Sections: Discovery, Analysis, Roadmap Development, Client Review, Admin
  - Milestones: Stakeholder interviews complete (Week 4), Current state assessment (Week 6), Draft roadmap (Week 10), Final delivery (Week 12)
  - Task dependencies mapped
- [ ] **Review intake questionnaire responses thoroughly** (you)
  - Highlight areas of ambiguity or potential conflict
  - Note discrepancies between what was in the proposal and new information
  - Prepare 3-5 follow-up questions for the kickoff
- [ ] **Research the client's industry context** (you)
  - Logistics industry digital maturity benchmarks
  - Common transformation patterns in mid-size logistics (WMS, TMS, IoT, data analytics)
  - Competitor landscape: who has transformed successfully?
  - Regulatory considerations (EU supply chain regulations, data protection)
- [ ] **Design stakeholder interview plan** (you)
  - Identify 6-10 interviewees across: C-suite, operations, IT, finance, frontline managers
  - Draft interview guide with role-specific questions
  - Plan 45-60 minute sessions, 2-3 per day maximum
  - Allocate 2 weeks for interviews
- [ ] **Draft kickoff agenda** (you) — send to client's project sponsor 24 hours before
- [ ] **Prepare a one-page engagement overview** (you)
  - Timeline, milestones, deliverables, communication plan
  - Visual enough to be shared with the leadership team

---

## Phase 3: Kickoff (Day 3-5)

**Goal:** Align with the project sponsor, meet key stakeholders, and lock in the interview schedule.

- [ ] **Confirm kickoff attendees** (you → client)
  - Ideal: CEO/Managing Director + CTO/IT Director + Operations Director + project sponsor
  - This is the steering committee for the engagement
- [ ] **Run kickoff meeting (60 min max)** (you facilitates)
  - Introductions and your relevant experience in logistics/transformation (5 min)
  - Their vision: "What does this company look like in 3 years?" — let them talk (15 min)
  - Present engagement methodology: Discovery → Analysis → Roadmap (10 min)
  - Walk through timeline and milestones (10 min)
  - Discuss communication cadence: biweekly steering committee, weekly email update (5 min)
  - Interview logistics: who, when, where, confidentiality assurance (10 min)
  - Questions and immediate concerns (5 min)
- [ ] **Send kickoff summary within 24 hours** (you)
  - Confirmed engagement goals (in their words, validated by them)
  - Steering committee members and roles
  - Interview schedule (or timeline for scheduling)
  - Key decisions and action items with owners
  - Next touchpoint: first steering committee meeting (Week 2)
- [ ] **Share Asana board with project sponsor** (you)
  - Read-only or comment-only access — they see progress, you manage tasks
- [ ] **Send interview scheduling requests** (you → client's EA or project sponsor to coordinate)
  - Provide a brief description of each interview's purpose
  - Suggest in-person interviews in Hamburg for richer data (offer video as backup)

---

## Phase 4: First Delivery (Day 10-14)

**Goal:** Deliver a preliminary findings document after the first wave of interviews.

- [ ] **Complete first 4-6 stakeholder interviews** (you) — by Day 10
  - Start with the C-suite and IT leadership
  - Record interviews (with permission) for accurate reference
  - Take structured notes: pain points, aspirations, perceived barriers, quick wins
- [ ] **Compile interview themes document** (you) — deliver by Day 12-14
  - NOT a transcript — a synthesized view of what you heard
  - Organized by theme: technology gaps, process inefficiencies, cultural readiness, data maturity
  - Highlight areas of alignment (everyone agrees) and tension (leadership disagrees)
  - Include 3-5 emerging hypotheses for the client to react to
- [ ] **Send preliminary findings to project sponsor first** (you)
  - Email with the document and a framing note:
    - "These are early observations after the first wave of interviews. I want to share them now so we can course-correct before I go deeper."
    - Ask: "Does this match your sense of the situation? Anything surprising? Anything I'm missing?"
  - Offer a 30-minute call to discuss
- [ ] **Follow up if no response in 48 hours** (you)
  - "I want to make sure I'm heading in the right direction. Can we find 20 minutes this week to discuss the preliminary findings?"

---

## Phase 5: Check-In (Day 21-30)

**Goal:** Formal process review before entering the deeper analysis phase.

- [ ] **Schedule 30-minute check-in with project sponsor** (you) — around end of Week 3
- [ ] **Prepare check-in discussion points** (you)
  - Progress against plan: interviews completed, emerging themes, timeline status
  - Process feedback:
    - "Is the communication cadence working for you? Too much, too little?"
    - "Are the biweekly steering committee meetings the right forum?"
    - "Is there anyone else I should be talking to that we didn't initially identify?"
    - "Any concerns about the direction of the findings so far?"
  - Preview of the analysis phase: what to expect in weeks 5-8
- [ ] **Send first steering committee update** (you)
  - A structured 2-page update: progress, key findings to date, next steps, risks
  - Include a clear "What we need from you" section
  - Present at the steering committee meeting, then send the document
- [ ] **Adjust engagement approach based on feedback** (you)
  - Document any scope, process, or timeline changes
  - Confirm changes via email to the project sponsor
  - Update Asana milestones if timeline shifts

---

## Communication Cadence (Full Engagement)

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Format | Attendees |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Steering committee meeting | Biweekly (Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) | Video call, 45-60 min | Steering committee |
| Weekly status email | Weekly (Fridays) | Email | Project sponsor |
| Interview debrief | After each interview wave | Email summary | Project sponsor |
| Ad-hoc questions | As needed | Email | Project sponsor |
| Milestone deliveries | 3 times (Week 4, 8, 12) | Email + meeting | Steering committee |

## Key Dates Summary

| Milestone | Target | Owner |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Welcome email + intake sent | Day 0 | You |
| Intake + documentation received | Day 3 | Client |
| Kickoff meeting | Day 4-5 | Both |
| Stakeholder interviews begin | Day 7 | You |
| Preliminary findings shared | Day 12-14 | You |
| Process check-in | Day 21-25 | Both |
| All interviews complete | Week 4 | You |
| Current state assessment delivered | Week 6 | You |
| Second steering committee review | Week 8 | Both |
| Draft transformation roadmap | Week 10 | You |
| Final roadmap + presentation | Week 12 | You |
| Invoice: Month 2 | Day 30 | You |
| Invoice: Month 3 | Day 60 | You |

---

> **Note:** Consulting engagements live or die by stakeholder access. If interviews get delayed because leaders are "too busy," escalate to the project sponsor immediately. A two-week interview delay creates a four-week project delay because everything downstream depends on the discovery phase.
