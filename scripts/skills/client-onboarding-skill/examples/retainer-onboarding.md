# Example: Retainer Client Onboarding

## User Input

> I run a small design agency (me plus 2 designers) and we just signed a retainer client — a DTC skincare brand based in Berlin. They're paying €4,000/month for 40 hours of design work. Scope includes social media graphics, email templates, packaging tweaks, and occasional brand collateral. No fixed project — they send requests as needed. We use Figma for design, ClickUp for task management, and Slack for communication. The contract is 6 months with a 30-day cancellation clause.

---

## Generated Output

# Client Onboarding Checklist
**Client:** DTC Skincare Brand (Berlin)
**Engagement:** Design Retainer — 40 hours/month
**Budget:** €4,000/month | **Term:** 6 months (30-day cancellation)
**Your Team:** You + 2 designers

---

## Phase 1: Welcome (Day 0-1)

**Goal:** Set retainer expectations, collect brand assets, and establish the request workflow.

- [ ] **Send welcome email within 2 hours of contract signing** (you)
  - Reference the retainer specifically — emphasize this is a partnership, not a project
  - Outline the onboarding timeline: "This week we'll get set up, next week we're fully operational"
  - Attach the brand intake questionnaire
  - Include a Slack channel invitation
  - Explain the request process at a high level: "You'll submit requests via ClickUp, we'll prioritize together, and you'll see designs in Figma"
- [ ] **Send brand intake questionnaire** (you) — retainer-specific questions:
  - Share your complete brand guidelines (or whatever exists)
  - Upload logo files in all formats (SVG, PNG, AI)
  - Provide your color palette (hex codes, Pantone if applicable)
  - List your approved fonts (with license files if needed)
  - Share your product photography library or image sources
  - What's your brand voice? (3-5 adjectives that describe how you communicate)
  - Who are your top 3 competitors visually?
  - Share examples of recent designs you loved and recent designs that missed the mark
  - What content types do you need most frequently? (Rank: social posts, email, packaging, ads, other)
  - Who on your team will submit requests? Who approves final designs?
  - What's your typical turnaround expectation? (24 hours, 48 hours, 1 week?)
- [ ] **Request platform access** (you)
  - Social media accounts (for asset specs and brand context)
  - Email platform (Klaviyo, Mailchimp — for template specs)
  - Shopify or e-commerce backend (for product images and brand context)
  - Any existing Canva, Figma, or design file repositories
  - Photo/asset libraries (Dropbox, Google Drive)
- [ ] **Schedule kickoff meeting for Day 2-3** (you)
  - Retainer kickoffs should happen fast — the client is already paying

---

## Phase 2: Setup (Day 1-2)

**Goal:** Build the infrastructure for a smooth, ongoing design operation.

- [ ] **Create ClickUp workspace for the retainer** (you)
  - List structure:
    - **Incoming Requests** (client submits here)
    - **In Progress** (your team is working)
    - **Client Review** (awaiting feedback)
    - **Approved** (done, archived monthly)
    - **On Hold / Backlog** (parked requests)
  - Custom fields: Request type, Priority (Urgent/High/Normal), Due date, Hours estimate
  - Create a request template with required fields: description, dimensions/specs, reference images, deadline, approver
- [ ] **Create Slack channel: #skincare-design** (you)
  - Invite the client's team (request submitters + approver)
  - Invite your 2 designers
  - Pin a message with: ClickUp request link, Figma project link, and "How to submit a request" summary
- [ ] **Set up Figma project** (you)
  - Create a shared Figma project: "[Client Name] — Retainer"
  - Organize pages: Social Media, Email, Packaging, Brand Collateral, Archive
  - Upload brand assets as a shared library (colors, fonts, logo, components)
  - Grant client "can view + comment" access (not edit, unless they request it)
- [ ] **Review brand assets and intake responses** (you)
  - Identify gaps: missing logo formats, incomplete color palette, no typography hierarchy
  - Flag these for the kickoff — don't chase down assets one by one over Slack
- [ ] **Assign internal team roles** (you)
  - Designer A: primary on social media and email templates
  - Designer B: primary on packaging and brand collateral
  - You: creative direction, client communication, quality review
- [ ] **Draft kickoff agenda and send to client** (you)
- [ ] **Create a "Monthly Hours Tracker" document** (you)
  - Transparent tracking of hours used vs. hours available
  - Shared with client monthly (or accessible in real-time via ClickUp time tracking)

---

## Phase 3: Kickoff (Day 2-3)

**Goal:** Align on the workflow, introduce the team, and establish the retainer rhythm.

- [ ] **Confirm kickoff attendees** (you → client)
  - Their side: marketing manager (daily contact) + founder/creative director (approver)
  - Your side: you + both designers (introduce the team they'll work with)
- [ ] **Run kickoff meeting (45 min)** (you facilitates)
  - Introductions: who does what on both sides (5 min)
  - Brand deep dive: walk through their brand guidelines together, ask questions (10 min)
  - Workflow walkthrough: live demo of ClickUp request process (10 min)
    - "Here's how you submit a request"
    - "Here's how we prioritize and estimate hours"
    - "Here's where you review and approve in Figma"
  - Turnaround expectations (5 min)
    - Standard request: 3-5 business days
    - Rush request: 24-48 hours (counts as 1.5x hours)
    - Define what "rush" means — not everything is urgent
  - Monthly rhythm: planning call, hours report, retainer review (5 min)
  - First requests: "What do you need this week?" (5 min)
  - Questions and concerns (5 min)
- [ ] **Send kickoff summary within 24 hours** (you)
  - Workflow confirmed: how to request, turnaround times, revision process
  - Team roles on both sides
  - First batch of requests logged in ClickUp
  - Monthly planning call scheduled (first Monday or Friday of each month)
  - Reminder: 40 hours/month, unused hours don't roll over (or do — per contract)
- [ ] **Invite client to Figma project and ClickUp** (you)
- [ ] **Log first batch of requests in ClickUp** (you)
  - Assign to designers with estimated hours and due dates

---

## Phase 4: First Delivery (Day 4-8)

**Goal:** Deliver the first batch of designs to set the quality bar and establish the feedback loop.

- [ ] **Complete first 2-3 design requests** (your team) — deliver by Day 5-7
  - Prioritize the request the client mentioned is most urgent at kickoff
  - Over-invest in quality on the first delivery — this sets expectations for the engagement
  - Ensure designs follow the brand guidelines exactly
- [ ] **Present first designs in Figma with a Slack message** (you or assigned designer)
  - Link to the Figma page with designs
  - Brief context for each: "Here's the Instagram carousel for the new serum launch. We went with a clean layout that highlights the product photography — let us know if the text hierarchy feels right."
  - Tag the approver: "When you're ready, please leave comments directly in Figma or give us the thumbs up."
  - Clarify revision scope: "This includes one round of revisions. Major direction changes count as a new request."
- [ ] **Track hours transparently** (you)
  - Log hours per request in ClickUp
  - After the first batch, send a quick summary: "First week: 12 of 40 hours used across 3 requests"
- [ ] **Follow up on feedback within 48 hours** (you)
  - If no response: "Hi [name], just checking in on the designs. Want to make sure we have time for revisions before [deadline]."
- [ ] **Process feedback and deliver revisions** (your team) — within 24-48 hours of receiving feedback

---

## Phase 5: Check-In (Day 21-30)

**Goal:** Review the first month's retainer performance and adjust the workflow.

- [ ] **Prepare Month 1 retainer report** (you)
  - Total requests completed
  - Hours used vs. hours available (with breakdown by request type)
  - Average turnaround time
  - Number of revision rounds per request
  - Highlight: what went well, what could improve
- [ ] **Run first monthly planning + review call (30 min)** (you + client)
  - Review the Month 1 report together (5 min)
  - Process feedback (10 min):
    - "How is the request process working? Easy enough?"
    - "Are turnaround times meeting your expectations?"
    - "Is the design quality hitting the mark?"
    - "Is 40 hours enough, too much, or about right?"
    - "Anything you'd change about how we work together?"
  - Plan Month 2 priorities (10 min):
    - Upcoming campaigns, launches, or seasonal content
    - Any new request types or design needs
    - Capacity planning: even distribution or front-loaded?
  - Open discussion (5 min)
- [ ] **Adjust process based on feedback** (you)
  - Update ClickUp templates if request fields need changing
  - Adjust turnaround expectations if they were unrealistic
  - Reassign designer roles if certain work types need different skills
  - Send a follow-up email confirming changes
- [ ] **Set up recurring monthly planning call** (you)
  - Same time each month, calendar invite for the full 6-month term

---

## Retainer-Specific Workflow Rules

> These rules govern the ongoing retainer beyond onboarding. Include them in the kickoff summary.

1. **Request submission:** All requests go through ClickUp. Slack messages are for discussion, not briefs. If a client sends a request via Slack, reply: "Got it — can you drop this into ClickUp so we can track it properly? Here's the link."
2. **Hour tracking:** Hours are tracked per request. The client receives a summary at month-end. If the retainer is trending over 40 hours by mid-month, notify the client and discuss priorities.
3. **Rollover policy:** Per contract (confirm: do unused hours roll over?). If not, proactively suggest ways to use remaining hours in the last week of the month.
4. **Scope boundaries:** The retainer covers design execution. Strategy, copywriting, photography direction, and print production coordination are out of scope unless otherwise agreed.
5. **Rush requests:** Anything needed in under 48 hours counts as rush. Rush work is billed at 1.5x hours. The client must approve the rush designation before work begins.
6. **Revision policy:** Each request includes 1 round of revisions. Additional rounds count toward monthly hours.
7. **Quality gate:** Every design goes through your creative review before the client sees it. Designers do not send directly to the client.

---

> **Note:** Retainers fail when they become transactional — request in, design out, repeat. Prevent this by treating the monthly planning call as sacred. It's where you shift from vendor to partner. Proactively suggest design improvements, flag brand inconsistencies, and bring ideas the client didn't ask for. That's what keeps a retainer renewing past month 6.
