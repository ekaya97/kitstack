## Understanding & Context

Premium plant retail is experiencing a shift. Your competitors are splitting into two camps: mass-market (IKEA, Dehner, Amazon) competing on price and convenience, and premium boutiques competing on curation, expertise, and aesthetics. Bloom & Grow belongs firmly in the second camp — but your current visual identity and lack of digital presence limits your reach to walk-in traffic.

The e-commerce opportunity isn't about replacing your physical store. It's about extending your reach to customers who discovered you on Instagram but live outside your delivery radius, and capturing the gift market (plants as gifts need beautiful presentation and reliable delivery — areas where generic platforms fail).

Your existing brand has equity. We're not starting from zero — we're evolving what works (the warmth, the expertise, the curation) into a system that's consistent across physical and digital touchpoints.

---

## Approach

We work in four phases. Each phase has its own deliverables and approval point — you'll never be surprised by a direction you didn't agree to.

**Brand Foundation (Weeks 1-2):** We start with your story. Through workshops and research, we define what Bloom & Grow stands for, who your ideal customer is, and how you're different from every other plant shop. This becomes the strategic foundation for everything visual and verbal.

**Visual Identity (Weeks 3-5):** We design the complete brand system — logo, colors, typography, photography direction, packaging concepts. Everything is designed as a system, not individual pieces, so your brand feels cohesive whether someone sees your Instagram, walks into your shop, or unboxes a delivery.

**Shopify Build (Weeks 5-9):** Development runs in parallel with the final brand phase. We build a custom Shopify theme that reflects the new brand — not a template with your colors swapped in. Product pages, care guides, the gift experience, checkout flow — all tailored.

**Launch (Week 10):** Content upload, QA, soft launch to your email list, then full public launch. We stick around for the first week post-launch to squash bugs and optimize based on real customer behavior.

## Scope of Work

### Phase 1: Brand Foundation (Weeks 1-2) — EUR 4,200

**Deliverables:**
- Brand workshop (3 hours, in-person or video) — values, audience, positioning, competitive differentiation
- Customer archetype profiles (2 primary, 1 secondary)
- Brand positioning statement and messaging pillars
- Verbal identity: tone of voice guidelines, naming conventions for product categories

**You'll need to provide:** Access to your sales data (what sells, to whom), your top 10 customer stories/testimonials, any existing brand materials.

### Phase 2: Visual Identity (Weeks 3-5) — EUR 7,600

**Deliverables:**
- Logo system: primary wordmark, secondary mark, icon (for app icons, favicons, labels)
- Color palette: 2 primary, 3 secondary, 2 neutrals — formulated for print, digital, and packaging
- Typography: heading and body typefaces, sizing scale
- Photography art direction: style guide for product photography, lifestyle imagery, and social content
- Illustration/icon style: 12 custom icons for plant care, delivery, categories
- Brand guidelines document (40+ pages, delivered as PDF and Figma file)
- Packaging concept: shipping box, tissue paper, plant care card, sticker — design files ready for print production

**Approval process:** We present 2 visual directions in Week 3. You select one. We refine in Week 4. Final approval by end of Week 5.

### Phase 3: Shopify E-Commerce Build (Weeks 5-9) — EUR 9,800

**Deliverables:**
- Custom Shopify theme (not a modified template — built to your brand system)
- Page templates designed and built:
  - Homepage with seasonal collections, bestsellers, and editorial content
  - Collection pages with filtering (plant type, light requirements, pet-safe, difficulty)
  - Product pages with care guides, size options, pot add-ons
  - Gift builder: select plant + pot + card + wrapping → single gift SKU
  - About page with your story and team
  - Care guide hub (SEO-optimized, drives organic traffic)
  - Contact / store locator
- Functional features:
  - Shopify checkout with Klarna and PayPal integration
  - Shipping zones (local delivery, Germany-wide, EU)
  - Basic inventory management setup
  - Email automations: order confirmation, shipping notification, care guide follow-up (7 days post-delivery)
  - Newsletter signup with welcome discount
- Mobile-first responsive design (70%+ of plant shop traffic is mobile)
- Performance optimization: target 90+ Lighthouse score
- Basic SEO setup: meta tags, schema markup, sitemap

**You'll need to provide:** Product list with descriptions, pricing, and photos (we'll advise on photography but studio shoots are not included). Shipping rates and zones. Payment processing account (Shopify Payments or Stripe).

### Phase 4: Launch & Handoff (Week 10) — EUR 2,200

**Deliverables:**
- Content upload and QA (up to 50 initial products)
- Cross-browser and device testing
- Soft launch to your email list (Monday)
- Full public launch (Thursday)
- Post-launch monitoring and bug fixes (5 business days)
- Shopify admin training session (90 minutes, recorded)
- Handoff documentation: how to add products, manage orders, update content
