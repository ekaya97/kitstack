## Understanding & Context

B2B SaaS companies expanding from the US to Europe face a specific set of challenges that domestic growth doesn't prepare them for: fragmented markets with distinct buying behaviors, GDPR and data residency requirements, localization beyond simple translation, and channel dynamics that vary significantly between the UK, DACH, and Nordics.

At $8M ARR, NovaTech is at the right inflection point for international expansion — large enough to absorb the investment, small enough to be nimble in market entry. The key risk at this stage isn't whether to expand, but where to start and how to sequence markets to build momentum without spreading resources too thin.

Your current product-market fit in the domestic market gives us a strong foundation. The strategic question is which segments of that fit translate to European buyers, and where the gaps are that need addressing before launch.

---

## Approach & Methodology

Our approach follows a proven three-phase framework for SaaS international expansion. Each phase builds on the previous one, with decision gates that let you validate direction before committing to the next phase.

**Phase 1 isolates the right market.** Rather than defaulting to the UK (the easy but increasingly crowded choice), we'll evaluate 5-7 European markets against your specific product characteristics, competitive landscape, and resource constraints. We'll conduct 12-15 interviews with potential European buyers, partners, and industry analysts to pressure-test our desk research with real market feedback.

**Phase 2 builds the playbook.** Once we've selected 1-2 priority markets, we'll develop the complete go-to-market strategy: positioning adjustments, pricing (including currency and purchasing power parity), channel strategy, and the regulatory checklist you'll need to address before launch.

**Phase 3 creates the execution plan.** Strategy without execution planning is a PDF that gathers dust. We'll deliver a 12-month roadmap with quarterly OKRs, budget allocation, hiring plan, and partnership targets — specific enough that your team can start executing on day one after the engagement ends.

## Scope of Work

### Phase 1: Market Assessment & Selection (Weeks 1-3)

| Deliverable | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Market screening matrix | 5-7 European markets scored on market size, competitive density, regulatory complexity, cultural fit, and NovaTech-specific factors |
| Buyer research | 12-15 interviews with target-profile buyers, channel partners, and industry analysts in priority markets |
| Competitive landscape | Mapping of incumbent and emerging competitors in each market, including local players not visible from the US |
| Market selection report | Data-backed recommendation of 1-2 priority markets with rationale |
| Decision gate | 60-minute presentation to NovaTech leadership team |

**Client dependencies:** Access to your ICP definition, current customer data (firmographics, usage patterns), and introductions to any existing European contacts.

### Phase 2: Go-to-Market Strategy (Weeks 4-6)

| Deliverable | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Positioning & messaging | Adjusted value proposition for European buyers, competitive differentiation in local context |
| Pricing strategy | Currency strategy, PPP-adjusted pricing, local payment preferences, VAT/tax implications |
| Channel strategy | Direct vs. partner vs. hybrid, target partner profiles, channel economics |
| Regulatory checklist | GDPR compliance gaps, data residency requirements, local legal entity considerations |
| Localization roadmap | Product, content, and support localization priorities (what to localize first vs. later) |

**Client dependencies:** Access to your product team (2 x 60-min sessions on localization feasibility), current pricing model and unit economics.

### Phase 3: Execution Roadmap (Weeks 7-8)

| Deliverable | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| 12-month execution plan | Quarterly OKRs, workstreams, owners, dependencies |
| Budget model | Investment required for Year 1 European operations (hiring, marketing, legal, infrastructure) |
| Hiring plan | Roles, timing, and location recommendations for first 3-5 European hires |
| Partnership playbook | Target partner list, outreach sequence, partnership terms framework |
| KPI dashboard | Metrics framework for tracking European market traction |

**Client dependencies:** Budget parameters for Year 1 European investment, hiring constraints, partnership preferences.
