# Scope Templates

Pre-built scope sections for 8 common project types. Use as starting points and customize based on the specific engagement.

---

## 1. Brand Strategy & Identity

### Phase 1: Discovery & Audit (2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Stakeholder interview sessions (up to 4 interviews, 45 min each)
- Competitive landscape analysis (5-8 competitors)
- Brand perception audit (internal + external)
- Discovery findings report with strategic recommendations

**Client dependencies:** Access to stakeholders, existing brand assets, customer data

### Phase 2: Strategy & Positioning (2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Brand positioning statement
- Brand architecture document (if multi-brand)
- Messaging framework: tagline, value propositions, tone of voice guidelines
- Target audience personas (3-4 personas)

**Client dependencies:** Feedback on positioning options within 3 business days

### Phase 3: Visual Identity (3-4 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Logo system (primary, secondary, icon)
- Color palette (primary, secondary, accent)
- Typography system (headings, body, accent)
- Photography/illustration style direction
- Brand guidelines document (30-50 pages)

**Client dependencies:** 2 rounds of feedback on visual concepts

### Phase 4: Implementation & Rollout (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Business card and stationery templates
- Email signature template
- Social media profile assets
- Presentation template (PowerPoint/Google Slides)
- Brand launch brief

**Out of scope:** Website redesign, packaging design, environmental/signage design, brand video production

## 2. Web Design & Development

### Phase 1: Discovery & Planning (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Requirements documentation
- Information architecture (sitemap)
- Content audit and migration plan (if redesign)
- Technical requirements specification
- Project plan with milestones

**Client dependencies:** Access to current site analytics, existing content inventory

### Phase 2: UX Design (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- User flow diagrams for key journeys
- Wireframes for all unique page templates (typically 8-15 templates)
- Mobile and desktop responsive layouts
- Interactive prototype for key flows

**Client dependencies:** 1 round of wireframe feedback within 5 business days

### Phase 3: Visual Design (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- High-fidelity designs for all page templates
- Design system/component library
- Responsive breakpoint specifications
- Animation and interaction specifications

**Client dependencies:** 2 rounds of design feedback. Final sign-off before development begins.

### Phase 4: Development (3-6 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Front-end development (HTML/CSS/JS or framework)
- CMS integration and content entry
- Contact forms and integrations (email, CRM)
- SEO implementation (meta tags, schema, sitemap)
- Cross-browser testing (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
- Mobile responsiveness testing
- Performance optimization (target: 90+ Lighthouse score)

**Client dependencies:** Final content delivered before development begins. CMS training attendance.

### Phase 5: Launch & Handoff (1 week)
**Deliverables:**
- Staging site review and QA
- DNS migration and go-live
- Post-launch monitoring (72 hours)
- CMS training session (1 hour recorded)
- Documentation: login credentials, hosting details, maintenance guide

**Out of scope:** Ongoing content creation, SEO strategy/content marketing, custom integrations beyond specified, e-commerce functionality (unless specified), ongoing hosting and maintenance

## 3. Marketing Campaign

### Phase 1: Strategy & Planning (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Campaign brief (objectives, KPIs, target audience, channels)
- Audience segmentation and targeting plan
- Channel strategy with budget allocation
- Campaign timeline and content calendar
- Measurement framework (what we'll track, how, when)

**Client dependencies:** Marketing budget confirmation, brand guidelines, access to analytics platforms

### Phase 2: Creative Development (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Key messaging and copy platform
- Ad creative concepts (3 directions, 1 selected for production)
- Ad variations per channel (sizes/formats as specified)
- Landing page copy and wireframe
- Email sequences (if applicable)

**Client dependencies:** Creative approval within 5 business days. 1 round of revisions included.

### Phase 3: Production & Setup (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Final ad assets in all required sizes/formats
- Landing page development
- Ad platform setup (targeting, bidding, tracking pixels)
- UTM parameter structure
- A/B test setup (minimum 2 variants per channel)

**Client dependencies:** Ad platform access, tracking pixel installation on client's site

### Phase 4: Launch & Optimization (4-8 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Campaign launch and monitoring
- Weekly performance reports
- Bi-weekly optimization adjustments (budget reallocation, creative refresh, audience refinement)
- Final campaign report with results, learnings, and recommendations

**Out of scope:** Media spend (billed separately by platforms), influencer partnerships, PR activities, organic social media management, video production beyond specified

## 4. Content Strategy

### Phase 1: Audit & Research (2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Content audit of existing materials (up to 100 pieces)
- Competitor content analysis (5 competitors)
- Keyword and topic research (SEO opportunity map)
- Audience research and content preferences analysis

### Phase 2: Strategy Development (2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Content strategy document: goals, audience, channels, content types, frequency
- Editorial guidelines: tone of voice, style guide, content standards
- Content pillar framework (4-6 pillars with topic clusters)
- Distribution strategy: owned, earned, paid channel mix

### Phase 3: Content Calendar & Templates (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- 3-month editorial calendar with topics, formats, channels, and owners
- Content brief template (for internal or freelance writers)
- Content production workflow (ideation → draft → review → publish → distribute)
- Measurement dashboard setup (Google Analytics, social analytics)

**Out of scope:** Ongoing content creation, content writing, social media management, paid media management, SEO implementation

## 5. Product Design (UX/UI)

### Phase 1: Research & Discovery (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- User research plan and execution (5-8 user interviews)
- User personas (3-4 primary personas)
- User journey maps for key workflows
- Competitive UX analysis (5-8 competitors)
- Research findings report with design implications

### Phase 2: Information Architecture & Wireframes (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Product structure and navigation model
- Wireframes for all key screens/flows
- Interactive wireframe prototype (Figma)
- Usability testing with wireframes (5 participants)
- Iteration based on test findings

### Phase 3: Visual Design System (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Design system: colors, typography, spacing, iconography
- Component library in Figma (buttons, inputs, cards, modals, tables, etc.)
- High-fidelity mockups for all key screens
- Responsive specifications (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Interaction and animation specifications

### Phase 4: Prototype & Handoff (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Interactive high-fidelity prototype
- Developer handoff documentation (Figma dev mode or Zeplin)
- Design QA support during development (up to X hours)
- Design system documentation for internal team

**Out of scope:** Front-end or back-end development, user testing recruitment, ongoing design iterations post-handoff

## 6. Management Consulting

### Phase 1: Diagnostic (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Stakeholder interviews (up to 8 interviews)
- Data collection and analysis (financial, operational, market)
- Current state assessment
- Diagnostic findings presentation

### Phase 2: Analysis & Recommendations (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Strategic options analysis (3-4 options evaluated)
- Financial modeling and scenario analysis
- Recommended strategy with rationale
- Implementation roadmap (high-level)
- Executive presentation (board-ready)

### Phase 3: Implementation Planning (2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Detailed implementation plan with workstreams, owners, timelines
- Resource requirements and budget estimate
- Risk register with mitigation strategies
- Quick-win identification (90-day action plan)
- Change management considerations

**Out of scope:** Ongoing implementation support (available as separate retainer), organizational restructuring execution, technology selection or implementation, recruitment

## 7. Technology Implementation

### Phase 1: Requirements & Architecture (2-3 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Business requirements document
- Technical requirements specification
- System architecture document
- Integration mapping (APIs, data flows)
- Technology selection recommendation (if applicable)
- Project plan with sprint structure

### Phase 2: Build — Core (4-8 weeks, in sprints)
**Deliverables:**
- Core functionality development per sprint backlog
- Database design and implementation
- API development
- Authentication and authorization
- Sprint demos (bi-weekly) with client feedback
- Automated test coverage (target: 80%+)

### Phase 3: Build — Integrations & Polish (2-4 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Third-party integrations (as specified)
- Data migration (if applicable)
- Performance optimization
- Security audit and hardening
- User acceptance testing support

### Phase 4: Launch & Stabilization (1-2 weeks)
**Deliverables:**
- Deployment to production
- Monitoring and alerting setup
- Post-launch bug fixing (2-week warranty period)
- Technical documentation
- Knowledge transfer session with client's team
- Maintenance and support plan proposal

**Out of scope:** Ongoing maintenance and hosting (available as retainer), content creation, third-party software licensing costs, hardware procurement

## 8. Ongoing Advisory / Retainer

### Monthly Scope (adjust hours/deliverables to client)
**Included in monthly retainer:**
- X hours of strategic advisory calls (scheduled bi-weekly or weekly)
- Async support via email/Slack (response within 1 business day)
- Monthly strategic review and recommendations document
- Ad-hoc deliverable reviews and feedback (up to X per month)
- Quarterly business review with performance metrics

### Not included in retainer (billed as add-on projects):
- New project execution (strategy, design, development)
- Team training or workshops
- Vendor evaluation and selection
- Recruitment support
- Travel (billed at cost + day rate)

### Retainer terms:
- Minimum commitment: 3 months (6 months for executive advisory)
- Unused hours: do not roll over to next month
- Overage: additional hours billed at $X/hr with prior approval
- Quarterly review: scope and fee adjusted every 3 months based on evolving needs
- Termination: 30 days written notice after minimum term
