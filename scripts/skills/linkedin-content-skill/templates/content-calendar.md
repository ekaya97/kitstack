# Content Calendar Template

A 4-week posting plan skeleton. Customize based on the user's posting frequency, content pillars, and audience.

---

## Setup: Before Building the Calendar

### 1. Define Posting Frequency
- **Minimum viable:** 2x/week (Tuesday + Thursday)
- **Recommended:** 3x/week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)
- **High growth:** 5x/week (weekdays)
- **Maximum effective:** 5x/week. More than that creates fatigue for your audience.

### 2. Define Content Pillars (3-4 topics)
Content pillars are the 3-4 topics you want to be known for. Every post should fit within a pillar.

**Example pillars for a management consultant:**
- Pillar 1: Pricing & proposals (how consultants price and sell work)
- Pillar 2: Client management (relationship dynamics, scope management)
- Pillar 3: Industry insights (trends in consulting, changes in how companies buy services)
- Pillar 4: Personal journey (lessons from building a consulting practice)

**Example pillars for a SaaS founder:**
- Pillar 1: Product development (building, shipping, iterating)
- Pillar 2: Go-to-market (sales, marketing, positioning)
- Pillar 3: Team & culture (hiring, management, remote work)
- Pillar 4: Founder life (decisions, trade-offs, lessons)

### 3. Map Post Types to Days
Create a rhythm. The audience subconsciously expects certain types of content on certain days.

**Example mapping (3x/week):**
- Monday: Insight or Framework post (start the week with something smart)
- Wednesday: Story or How-I post (midweek personal/experiential content)
- Friday: Contrarian or Observation post (end the week with something provocative)

## 4-Week Calendar Template

### Week 1: Foundation

| Day | Post Type | Pillar | Topic Prompt | Notes |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Mon | Insight | [Pillar 1] | Share one non-obvious observation from your work this week | Focus on something you noticed, not something you advise |
| Wed | Story | [Pillar 4] | A specific moment that taught you something about your work | Must include real details: names, numbers, dates |
| Fri | Contrarian | [Pillar 2] | Challenge a common practice in your industry | Must include evidence from your experience |

### Week 2: Authority

| Day | Post Type | Pillar | Topic Prompt | Notes |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Mon | Framework | [Pillar 1] | Share a mental model or system you use regularly | Name the framework, show it in action |
| Wed | How-I | [Pillar 3] | Explain your specific approach to a common challenge | Include steps, results, and what you'd do differently |
| Fri | Data | [Pillar 2] | Share an interesting number from your work or industry | The number alone isn't the post — your interpretation is |

### Week 3: Connection

| Day | Post Type | Pillar | Topic Prompt | Notes |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Mon | Observation | [Pillar 3] | Something you noticed in your industry this week | Connect the observation to a broader trend |
| Wed | Mistake | [Pillar 4] | A professional failure and what you learned | Must be genuine. No humble brags. |
| Fri | Question | [Pillar 1] | Ask your audience a genuine question you want answered | Must be specific enough that the answers are interesting |

### Week 4: Impact

| Day | Post Type | Pillar | Topic Prompt | Notes |
|-----|-----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Mon | Insight | [Pillar 2] | Share the most useful thing you learned this month | Tie it to a specific experience, not abstract learning |
| Wed | Story | [Pillar 3] | A client interaction that changed your perspective | Focus on what you learned, not how great the outcome was |
| Fri | Contrarian | [Pillar 1] | What's one thing your industry gets wrong? | Include when the conventional wisdom IS right (nuance) |

## Weekly Content Workflow

### Monday: Plan (15 minutes)
- Review this week's calendar: post type, pillar, topic prompt
- Jot down 1-2 sentences of the core idea for each post
- Flag any posts that need research or data

### Tuesday-Thursday: Write (20 minutes each)
- Draft the post using the SKILL.md methodology
- Run through the anti-patterns checklist
- Review the hook — does it earn the click?
- Check voice consistency

### Posting Day: Publish + Engage (10 minutes)
- Post at the optimal time for your audience (typically 8-9 AM in their primary timezone)
- First 60 minutes after posting: respond to every comment. Early engagement signals quality to the algorithm.
- Do NOT post and disappear. The first hour matters most.

## Content Pipeline: Where Ideas Come From

Keep a running list of post ideas. Add to it throughout the week from these sources:

1. **Client conversations** — "A client asked me something today that made me think..." (anonymize as needed)
2. **Mistakes and surprises** — Something went wrong, or something unexpected worked
3. **Industry news** — Your reaction to a trend, report, or announcement
4. **Books and content you consume** — An insight from something you read, applied to your context
5. **Questions you get asked** — "I keep getting asked about [topic]. Here's my take."
6. **Data from your own work** — Track interesting metrics: win rates, time spent, results achieved
7. **Disagreements** — Something you read or heard that you disagree with (contrarian fuel)
8. **Before/after moments** — How you used to do something vs. how you do it now

**Rule:** Never post without a personal connection to the topic. If you can't trace the idea back to your own experience, research, or observation, it's not your post to write.

## Calendar Customization Notes

### For German-language audiences:
- Post in German if your audience is primarily DACH
- Reference German-specific contexts: Mittelstand, Steuerberater dynamics, German business culture
- Avoid direct translations of English LinkedIn trends — they often don't land the same way
- German LinkedIn tends to be more formal; calibrate accordingly

### For dual-language audiences:
- Pick one primary language and use it for 80% of posts
- Occasional posts in the secondary language for reach
- Never switch languages mid-post

### For high-frequency posting (5x/week):
Add to the template:
- Tuesday: How-I or Behind-the-Scenes (process content)
- Thursday: Curated Observation or Data post (analytical content)
This creates a rhythm: smart → personal → process → analytical → provocative

### For low-frequency posting (2x/week):
Keep only Tuesday and Thursday. Prioritize:
- Tuesday: Insight or Story (your strongest content type)
- Thursday: Contrarian or Framework (your most shareable content type)
