# Inkasso vs. Rechtsanwalt — When to Use Which

When your own dunning letters have failed, you have two main options for professional debt recovery: an Inkassounternehmen (debt collection agency) or a Rechtsanwalt (lawyer). This reference explains when to use which, the costs involved, and the legal boundaries.

## Overview

| Criteria | Inkassounternehmen | Rechtsanwalt |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Best for** | Clear, undisputed claims | Disputed claims, complex situations |
| **Cost structure** | Success-based or RVG-based | RVG (Rechtsanwaltsvergütungsgesetz) |
| **Legal authority** | Limited by RDG | Full legal representation |
| **Court representation** | No (must hand off to lawyer) | Yes, including Landgericht |
| **Psychological effect** | Strong for B2C, moderate for B2B | Very strong for all debtor types |
| **Speed** | Fast for standard cases | Variable, depends on case |
| **Volume capability** | Excellent for many small claims | Less efficient for bulk |

## When to Use Inkasso

**Ideal scenarios:**
- The claim is **clear and undisputed** — the debtor simply hasn't paid
- You have **many small claims** to collect (bulk efficiency)
- The debtor is a **consumer (B2C)** — the Inkasso letter often prompts immediate payment
- You want to **outsource the process** and focus on your business
- The amount is **moderate** (500-10,000 EUR) and doesn't justify lawyer fees

**How Inkasso works:**
1. You assign the claim to the Inkassounternehmen
2. They send their own dunning letters (on their letterhead — more intimidating)
3. They attempt to negotiate payment plans
4. If unsuccessful, they recommend filing a Mahnbescheid
5. For court proceedings, they must involve a lawyer

## When to Use a Rechtsanwalt

**Ideal scenarios:**
- The debtor **disputes the claim** (alleges defective work, wrong amount, etc.)
- The claim is **legally complex** (contract interpretation, warranty issues)
- The amount is **large** (over 5,000 EUR) — Landgericht requires a lawyer (Anwaltszwang)
- You need **court representation** beyond the Mahnverfahren
- The debtor has a lawyer — you need one too
- **Cross-border** claims or claims involving insolvency

## Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetz (RDG) — Legal Limits of Inkasso

The RDG (Legal Services Act) defines what Inkassounternehmen may and may not do:

**Inkasso MAY:**
- Send dunning letters and demand payment
- Negotiate payment plans
- File Mahnbescheid applications
- Report to credit agencies (Schufa, Creditreform)
- Accept payments on behalf of the creditor

**Inkasso MAY NOT:**
- Represent you in court proceedings (Klageverfahren)
- Provide legal advice beyond the scope of debt collection
- Represent you at the Landgericht (Anwaltszwang)
- Handle claims that require legal judgment (disputed claims)

## Cost Comparison

### Inkasso Costs

Inkasso fees are regulated by the RVG (same fee schedule as lawyers) for out-of-court collection:

| Claim Amount (EUR) | Inkasso Fee (1.3 Geschaeftsgebuehr) | Including USt (19%) |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------|
| 500 | 58.50 EUR | 69.62 EUR |
| 1,000 | 104.00 EUR | 123.76 EUR |
| 2,000 | 172.90 EUR | 205.75 EUR |
| 3,000 | 222.30 EUR | 264.54 EUR |
| 5,000 | 303.90 EUR | 361.64 EUR |
| 10,000 | 526.50 EUR | 626.54 EUR |
| 25,000 | 958.50 EUR | 1,140.62 EUR |

Note: These are the maximum fees the Inkasso company can charge the debtor. Some companies also charge the creditor a processing fee or work on success-based models (e.g., 10-25% of the collected amount).

**Who pays?** The debtor pays the Inkasso fees if the claim is justified. The creditor typically pays nothing upfront (for reputable Inkasso companies). If the claim fails, the creditor may owe a processing fee.

### Rechtsanwalt Costs

Lawyer fees follow the RVG:

| Claim Amount (EUR) | Out-of-Court (1.3 Gebuehr) | Court (1.3 + 1.2 Gebuehr) | Including USt |
|---------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|---------------|
| 500 | 58.50 EUR | 112.50 EUR | (+ 19%) |
| 1,000 | 104.00 EUR | 200.00 EUR | (+ 19%) |
| 2,000 | 172.90 EUR | 332.50 EUR | (+ 19%) |
| 5,000 | 303.90 EUR | 584.50 EUR | (+ 19%) |
| 10,000 | 526.50 EUR | 1,013.50 EUR | (+ 19%) |
| 25,000 | 958.50 EUR | 1,843.50 EUR | (+ 19%) |

**Who pays?** Out-of-court fees: the debtor pays if in Verzug. Court fees: the losing party pays (Kostenentscheidung).

### Cost Comparison for a 5,000 EUR Claim

| Cost Item | Inkasso | Rechtsanwalt |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Out-of-court fee | 303.90 EUR | 303.90 EUR |
| Mahnverfahren court fee | 127.00 EUR | 127.00 EUR |
| Court representation (if needed) | Must hire lawyer additionally | Already engaged |
| Total (if resolved out of court) | ~303.90 EUR | ~303.90 EUR |
| Total (if court needed) | ~303.90 + lawyer | ~584.50 EUR |

**Key insight:** For out-of-court collection, Inkasso and a lawyer cost the same (both use RVG). The difference is in capability: the lawyer can take the case to court seamlessly; the Inkasso company cannot.

## Decision Framework

```
Is the claim disputed?
├── Yes → Use a Rechtsanwalt
└── No → Is the amount over 5,000 EUR?
    ├── Yes → Consider Rechtsanwalt (Landgericht may be needed)
    └── No → Do you have many similar claims?
        ├── Yes → Inkasso (bulk efficiency)
        └── No → Is the debtor a consumer?
            ├── Yes → Inkasso (strong psychological effect)
            └── No (B2B) → Either works — Rechtsanwalt if relationship matters
```

## Red Flags: Choosing an Inkasso Company

Avoid Inkasso companies that:
- Charge upfront fees to the creditor for simple claims
- Guarantee a specific success rate (no one can guarantee payment)
- Are not registered with the local Amtsgericht (Registrierung nach §10 RDG)
- Demand exclusive assignment of the claim (Abtretung) without good reason
- Charge fees significantly above RVG rates

**Reputable Inkasso companies** are members of the BDIU (Bundesverband Deutscher Inkasso-Unternehmen) and work on a "no recovery, no fee" basis for the creditor.

## Practical Recommendation

For freelancers and small businesses:
1. **Always start with your own dunning sequence** (Zahlungserinnerung through letzte Mahnung)
2. **For undisputed claims under 5,000 EUR:** Inkasso or self-file Mahnbescheid via mahngerichte.de
3. **For disputed claims or amounts over 5,000 EUR:** Consult a Rechtsanwalt
4. **For many small claims:** Inkasso with bulk processing
5. **Always consider the economics:** If Inkasso/lawyer fees approach the claim amount, a write-off may be more practical
