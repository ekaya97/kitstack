# Gerichtliches Mahnverfahren — Mahnbescheid Guide

When dunning letters have failed and the debtor still refuses to pay, the next step is the gerichtliches Mahnverfahren (judicial dunning procedure). This is a fast, inexpensive court procedure to obtain an enforceable title without a full trial.

## When to File a Mahnbescheid

- All dunning letters have been sent and ignored (minimum: one formal Mahnung)
- The deadline in the letzte Mahnung has passed
- The claim is undisputed (the debtor has not raised substantive objections to the claim itself)
- The claim is for a specific monetary amount in EUR

**Do NOT use if:**
- The debtor disputes the claim on substance (e.g., "The work was defective")
- The claim is for non-monetary performance (e.g., delivery of goods)
- The debtor is outside Germany (use European Payment Order instead)
- The amount is very small (under 50 EUR) — costs may not justify the procedure

## How to File Online

### Step 1: Register at mahngerichte.de

- URL: https://www.online-mahnantrag.de
- Register with your personal data
- Available 24/7, processed by the central Mahngericht of your Bundesland

### Step 2: Fill in the Application (Mahnantrag)

**Required information:**
1. **Antragsteller (Applicant/Creditor):** Your full name, address, date of birth (or company name and registration)
2. **Antragsgegner (Defendant/Debtor):** Full name, address — must be the exact legal name
3. **Hauptforderung (Main claim):** Invoice amount, invoice number, invoice date
4. **Nebenforderungen (Ancillary claims):**
   - Verzugszinsen (specify rate and period)
   - Mahnpauschale (40 EUR for B2B)
   - Prior out-of-court costs (if any)
5. **Grund des Anspruchs (Basis of claim):** Brief description (e.g., "Dienstleistungsvertrag" or "Werkvertrag")
6. **Zustaendiges Gericht:** Determined automatically based on debtor's address

### Step 4: Court Issues the Mahnbescheid

- The court does **not** check whether your claim is justified — it only checks formal requirements
- Processing time: typically 1-3 weeks
- The Mahnbescheid is served to the debtor by the court (Zustellung)

## What Happens After the Mahnbescheid

### Scenario A: Debtor Pays
- Best case. The debtor pays the full amount including Verzugszinsen and court fees.
- Procedure is complete.

### Scenario B: Debtor Does Nothing (No Widerspruch)
- The debtor has **2 weeks** to file a Widerspruch (objection)
- If no Widerspruch is filed, you can apply for a **Vollstreckungsbescheid** (enforcement order)
- The Vollstreckungsbescheid is a full enforceable title — equivalent to a court judgment
- With the Vollstreckungsbescheid you can initiate **Zwangsvollstreckung** (enforcement):
  - Kontopfaendung (bank account seizure)
  - Gerichtsvollzieher (bailiff)
  - Lohnpfaendung (wage garnishment)

### Scenario C: Debtor Files Widerspruch (Objection)
- The debtor objects to the claim within 2 weeks
- The Widerspruch does NOT need to be substantiated — just filed
- The case is automatically transferred to a regular court proceeding (Klageverfahren)
- You must now prove your claim in a full trial
- Additional court fees apply (the remaining 2.5 Gebuehrensaetze)
- **Consider:** Is the claim strong enough for a full trial? Consult a lawyer.

### Scenario D: Debtor Files Partial Widerspruch
- The debtor objects to part of the claim (e.g., disputes the Verzugszinsen but not the main amount)
- For the undisputed part: proceed to Vollstreckungsbescheid
- For the disputed part: Klageverfahren

## Timeline Overview

```
Day 0:    File Mahnantrag online
Day 7-21: Court issues Mahnbescheid, serves it to debtor
Day 21-35: Debtor's 2-week Widerspruch period
           ├── No Widerspruch → Apply for Vollstreckungsbescheid
           │   └── Day 42-56: Vollstreckungsbescheid issued
           │       └── Zwangsvollstreckung possible
           └── Widerspruch filed → Case moves to Klageverfahren
               └── Full trial (months to years)
```

**Total time to Vollstreckungsbescheid (if no objection): 4-8 weeks**

## Costs You Can Recover

If the claim succeeds, the debtor pays:
1. The original invoice amount
2. Verzugszinsen
3. Mahnpauschale (B2B, 40 EUR)
4. Court fees for the Mahnverfahren
5. Lawyer fees (if you used a lawyer, based on RVG)

## Practical Tips

1. **Double-check the debtor's name and address.** If the Mahnbescheid is sent to the wrong address, the procedure fails. For companies: use the exact name from the Handelsregister.
2. **Calculate Verzugszinsen up to the filing date** and request "interest on the principal from [date] at [X] percentage points above the Basiszinssatz" for the ongoing period.
3. **Keep proof of your dunning letters.** If the debtor files Widerspruch and the case goes to trial, you'll need to show your pre-litigation efforts.
4. **Don't wait too long.** Claims become time-barred (verjaehrt) after 3 years from the end of the year in which the claim arose (§195, §199 BGB). For an invoice from March 2026, Verjaehrung occurs on 31.12.2029.
5. **Consider a lawyer for amounts over 5,000 EUR** — the Amtsgericht handles claims up to 5,000 EUR, the Landgericht handles larger amounts (Anwaltszwang applies at the Landgericht).

## Alternative: European Payment Order

For debtors in other EU member states, use the European Payment Order (Europaeischer Zahlungsbefehl) under Regulation (EC) No. 1896/2006. Similar process but cross-border. Filed at the same court.
