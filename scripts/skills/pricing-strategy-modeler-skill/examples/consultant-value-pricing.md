# Example: Consultant Building a Value-Based Pricing Model

## User Input

> I'm an independent marketing consultant in Munich. I specialize in conversion rate optimization for e-commerce companies. I've been charging €140/hr for 6 years, but my last client told me I saved them €400,000 in the first year by improving their checkout flow. I feel like I'm leaving money on the table. I want to explore value-based pricing but I'm not sure how to structure it. My monthly costs are about €3,200. I usually work with companies doing €2-20M in annual revenue.

---

## Generated Output

# Pricing Analysis: Value-Based Pricing for CRO Consultant

**Current Rate:** €140/hr | **Location:** Munich | **Experience:** 6 years
**Specialization:** Conversion Rate Optimization for E-commerce
**Client Profile:** E-commerce companies, €2-20M revenue

## 1. Current State Assessment

### The Numbers You Already Know

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Current hourly rate | €140/hr |
| Annual billable hours (est. 70% util) | ~1,288 hrs |
| Gross annual revenue | ~€180,000 |
| Annual fixed costs | €38,400 |
| Pre-tax income | €141,600 |
| After tax (~35%) | ~€92,000 net |

You're doing well financially under the hourly model. This isn't a survival problem — it's an optimization problem.

### The Value Gap

Your last client engagement:
- Your fee (estimated): 150-200 hours × €140 = **€21,000-€28,000**
- Value created for the client: **€400,000** in year 1
- Your capture rate: **5.3-7.0%** of value created

In professional services, a healthy value capture rate is **10-20%**. At 10% capture, that engagement should have been priced at €40,000. At 15%, it's €60,000. At 20%, it's €80,000.

**You left €12,000-€52,000 on the table in a single engagement.** Over a career of similar engagements, this compounds into hundreds of thousands in foregone revenue.

### Why Hourly Fails for CRO

Conversion rate optimization is the textbook case for value-based pricing because:

1. **The value is directly measurable.** A 0.5% improvement in conversion rate at €10M revenue is €50,000-€200,000/year depending on margins.
2. **Your expertise makes you fast.** After 6 years, you spot issues in hours that would take others weeks. Hourly pricing penalizes your pattern recognition.
3. **The impact compounds.** Your optimizations continue generating revenue for years after the engagement ends. Hourly pricing only captures the time you worked, not the ongoing value.
4. **The ROI is provable.** Unlike brand strategy or organizational consulting, CRO has clear before/after metrics.

## 2. Value-Based Pricing Framework for CRO

### The Value Discovery Process

Before quoting any price, you need a structured discovery conversation. This is the most critical part of value-based pricing — without it, you're guessing.

**Discovery Questions (for CRO specifically):**

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| "What is your current annual revenue through e-commerce?" | Establishes the revenue base |
| "What is your current conversion rate?" | Baseline for improvement calculation |
| "What is your average order value?" | Needed for revenue impact modeling |
| "How much traffic do you get monthly?" | Determines improvement potential |
| "What's your gross margin on online sales?" | Determines actual profit impact (not just revenue) |
| "Have you done any optimization before? What happened?" | Gauges sophistication and past failures |
| "What would a 1% improvement in conversion rate mean in revenue?" | Gets THEM to do the math — more powerful than you saying it |
| "What's the lifetime value of a new customer?" | Often the real value is higher than a single purchase |
| "What's your customer acquisition cost?" | CRO reduces effective CAC — this is a second value layer |
| "How long would you expect optimizations to generate results?" | 12-24 months typical; this justifies your fee |

### Value Calculation Model

```
Step 1: Establish the revenue base
  Current monthly e-commerce revenue: [X]
  Current conversion rate: [Y%]

Step 2: Project a conservative improvement
  Conservative target: +15-30% relative improvement in conversion rate
  (e.g., 2.0% → 2.3-2.6%)

Step 3: Calculate annual revenue impact
  Additional monthly revenue = Current revenue × (Improvement / Current rate)
  Annual impact = Additional monthly revenue × 12

Step 4: Adjust for confidence and duration
  Year 1 confidence: 80% (conservative)
  Expected duration of impact: 2 years minimum

Step 5: Calculate your fee
  Adjusted annual value = Annual impact × 80%
  Capture rate: 10-15% of Year 1 adjusted value
  Your fee = Adjusted annual value × Capture rate
```

### Worked Example

| Metric | Client A (€5M) | Client B (€12M) | Client C (€20M) |
|--------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Annual e-commerce revenue | €5,000,000 | €12,000,000 | €20,000,000 |
| Current conversion rate | 1.8% | 2.2% | 2.5% |
| Target conversion rate (+20%) | 2.16% | 2.64% | 3.0% |
| Additional annual revenue | €1,000,000 | €2,400,000 | €4,000,000 |
| Confidence adjustment (80%) | €800,000 | €1,920,000 | €3,200,000 |
| **Your fee at 10% capture** | **€80,000** | **€192,000** | **€320,000** |
| **Your fee at 15% capture** | **€120,000** | **€288,000** | **€480,000** |
| Est. hours of work | 120 | 180 | 250 |
| **Effective hourly rate (10%)** | **€667/hr** | **€1,067/hr** | **€1,280/hr** |

Even at 10% capture, your effective rate is 4-9x your current hourly rate.

## 3. Pricing Structures for CRO

### Option A: Fixed Value Fee

**Structure:** One price for the full engagement, based on the value calculation.

**Example proposal language:**
> "Based on your current €12M in e-commerce revenue and a conservative 20% improvement in conversion rate, this engagement is projected to generate €2.4M in additional annual revenue. Our fee for the full optimization program is €195,000."

**Pros:** Simple, clean, maximum upside for you.
**Cons:** Client carries all the risk. Requires high trust and a strong track record.

**Best for:** Clients who know you, referrals, companies with sophisticated buying processes.

### Option B: Base + Performance Fee

**Structure:** A lower base fee plus a percentage of measured improvement.

```
Base fee: €45,000 (covers your time and methodology regardless of outcome)
Performance fee: 8% of incremental revenue above baseline for 12 months
Cap: €200,000 total (base + performance)
```

**Example scenario:**
- Baseline monthly revenue: €1,000,000
- Post-optimization monthly revenue: €1,200,000
- Incremental annual revenue: €2,400,000
- Performance fee: €2,400,000 × 8% = €192,000
- Total fee: €45,000 + €192,000 = **€237,000** (capped at €200,000)

**Pros:** Client risk is reduced. Your interests are aligned with theirs. The cap reassures them.
**Cons:** Requires tracking infrastructure. Revenue attribution can be disputed. Cash flow is delayed.

**Best for:** New relationships where trust is building. Clients who are skeptical of value claims.

### Option C: Tiered Engagement Model

**Structure:** Three tiers that map to different client sizes and scopes.

| Tier | Client Revenue | Scope | Duration | Investment |
|------|---------------|-------|----------|------------|
| **Optimize** | €2-5M | Checkout flow + 2 key pages | 6 weeks | €35,000-55,000 |
| **Accelerate** | €5-12M | Full funnel optimization (5-8 pages) | 10-12 weeks | €75,000-140,000 |
| **Transform** | €12-20M+ | Complete CRO program + team training | 4-6 months | €150,000-280,000 |

Each tier includes:
- Quantitative audit (analytics, heatmaps, session recordings)
- User research (surveys, interviews, usability testing)
- Hypothesis development and prioritization
- A/B test design and implementation support
- Results analysis and recommendations
- (Transform only) Internal team training and playbook

**Pros:** Decoy effect works here. Clients self-select into the tier that fits their budget and ambition.
**Cons:** You need to define clear scope differences between tiers.

**Best for:** Scaling your consulting practice. Marketing on your website.

## 4. Recommendation

**Start with Option B (Base + Performance) for your next 2-3 clients, then transition to Option A or C as your case studies grow.**

Here's why:
- You have one strong case study (€400K result). You need 3-5 more to confidently sell fixed value fees.
- Base + Performance de-risks the pitch for new clients while proving your ROI claims.
- After 2-3 successful performance-fee engagements, you'll have the data to quote fixed fees with confidence.

### Specific Starting Numbers

For your typical client (€5-12M e-commerce company):

| Component | Amount | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Base fee | €40,000-60,000 | Covers your time (~150-200 hours × €250-300/hr effective) |
| Performance fee | 6-10% of incremental revenue | Aligns incentives |
| Performance measurement period | 12 months post-implementation | Fair time for optimization to show results |
| Performance fee cap | 3x base fee | Protects the client relationship |
| **Expected total fee** | **€80,000-180,000** | Depending on results achieved |

## 5. Transition Plan

### Month 1: Case Study Development
- [ ] Document the €400K client result in detail: baseline metrics, what you did, timeline, results, methodology
- [ ] Request a testimonial and permission to use their name (or anonymized industry reference)
- [ ] Create a "results methodology" one-pager: how you measure and report CRO improvements
- [ ] Draft the new proposal template with value-based pricing structure

### Month 2: Sales Process Redesign
- [ ] Build a discovery call script (30-45 minutes) focused on value quantification
- [ ] Create a "CRO Value Calculator" — a simple spreadsheet you can share with prospects
- [ ] Update your website to lead with outcomes, not hourly rates: "Average client ROI: 15-30x investment"
- [ ] Prepare answers for pricing objections (see below)

### Month 3: First Value-Based Engagement
- [ ] Quote the next qualified prospect using Base + Performance structure
- [ ] Set up analytics and tracking infrastructure during the engagement (this is your proof)
- [ ] Document results weekly — this becomes your next case study
- [ ] After 3 months, share interim results with the client to build confidence

### Month 6+: Scaling
- [ ] With 3+ case studies, transition to fixed value fees (Option A) or tiered packages (Option C)
- [ ] Consider a paid discovery phase (€8,000-12,000) that produces a value assessment before quoting the full engagement
- [ ] Raise capture rates from 10% to 15% as your proof points strengthen

## 6. Handling the Sales Conversation

### The Pitch Flow

1. **Open with a question, not a pitch.** "Tell me about your e-commerce revenue and where you see the biggest friction in your customer journey."
2. **Quantify together.** "Your conversion rate is 2.1%. If we could move that to 2.5%, that's roughly €[X] in additional annual revenue. Does that math feel right to you?"
3. **Present your approach.** "Here's what the engagement looks like — 3 phases over 10 weeks."
4. **Present the price in context.** "The investment for this program is €95,000. Based on the projected €800,000 in additional revenue, that's an 8.4x return in the first year."
5. **Close with next steps.** "I'd start with a detailed audit of your checkout flow and top landing pages. If you'd like to move forward, I can have a proposal with the full scope by [date]."

### Handling Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "That's way more than our last consultant charged." | "What results did they deliver? My pricing is tied to outcomes, not hours. If I deliver the projected improvement, this fee is less than 10% of the revenue gain." |
| "How do we know you'll deliver the improvement?" | "That's exactly why I recommend the Base + Performance structure. My base fee covers the work regardless, and the performance fee only applies to measured results. You're not paying for promises." |
| "Can we start with a smaller project?" | "Absolutely. I offer a focused Optimize package — checkout flow and 2 key pages, 6 weeks, €35,000-55,000. It's a good way to see results before committing to a full program." |
| "We have an in-house team. Why not just hire someone?" | "A senior CRO specialist costs €80,000-100,000/year in salary plus benefits and tools. My engagement delivers results in 10-12 weeks and costs less than one year of an FTE. After the engagement, your team maintains the improvements." |
| "What if our revenue drops for other reasons?" | "We establish a clean baseline and isolate CRO impact using A/B testing — only changes attributable to our optimizations count. If your revenue drops due to market conditions, that's separate from our performance measurement." |
