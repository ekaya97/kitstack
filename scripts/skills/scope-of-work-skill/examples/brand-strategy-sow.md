## 1. Project Overview

PayBridge requires a complete brand foundation -- positioning, messaging architecture, and visual identity system -- to support its post-Series A growth in the DACH SMB market. The current brand assets (Canva-created logo, no guidelines) are insufficient for the company's next stage: hiring, sales enablement, marketing campaigns, investor communications, and partner relationships.

This engagement will produce a strategic brand platform that defines who PayBridge is, how it communicates, and how it looks -- delivered as a comprehensive brand guidelines document and ready-to-use asset package. The outcome is a brand system that works consistently across all touchpoints, from pitch decks to product UI to social media, without requiring the founding team to make ad-hoc design decisions.

---

## 2. Scope Definition

### 2.1 In Scope

1. **Brand research and discovery:** 6 stakeholder interviews (co-founders + key team members), competitive landscape analysis of 8-10 DACH fintech competitors, target audience research synthesis (using existing customer data and 4 customer interviews)
2. **Brand positioning:** Positioning statement, brand essence, brand values (3-5), brand personality definition, competitive differentiation framework
3. **Messaging architecture:** Core value proposition, audience-specific messaging (3 segments: micro-businesses, small businesses, mid-market SMBs), elevator pitch (15-second, 30-second, 60-second versions), tagline options (3 presented, 1 selected and refined)
4. **Verbal identity:** Tone of voice guidelines with spectrum definitions (e.g., "confident, not arrogant"), writing principles, vocabulary guidance (words to use, words to avoid), sample copy for key touchpoints (homepage hero, about page intro, social media bio)
5. **Visual identity -- logo system:** Primary logo, secondary/compact logo, icon/favicon, logo clear space and minimum size rules, logo usage guidelines (do's and don'ts), all delivered in SVG, PNG (transparent + white backgrounds), and EPS formats
6. **Visual identity -- design system:** Primary and secondary color palette with hex, RGB, and Pantone values; typography system (2 typefaces: headings and body) with scale and usage rules; spacing and layout principles; iconography style direction; photography/illustration style guidelines with example references
7. **Brand guidelines document:** 40-50 page comprehensive document covering all elements above, delivered as PDF and editable Figma file
8. **Core asset templates:** Business card, email signature, LinkedIn banner, presentation template (Google Slides, 20-slide master), one-pager/sales sheet template
9. **Brand launch brief:** Internal document for the PayBridge team explaining the new brand, how to use it, and the rollout plan

### 2.2 Out of Scope

- **Website design or development:** A website redesign based on the new brand can be scoped as a follow-on project
- **Product UI/UX design:** Application of brand to the PayBridge product interface is not included; UI guidelines can be developed separately
- **Marketing campaign strategy or execution:** No campaign planning, advertising, or content marketing
- **Copywriting beyond sample copy:** Full website copy, sales materials, or marketing content writing
- **Video or motion design:** No brand video, animated logo, or motion guidelines
- **Environmental / signage design:** No office branding, event materials, or physical signage
- **Packaging design:** Not applicable to PayBridge's current product
- **Ongoing design work:** Post-delivery design support available as a monthly retainer
- **Font licensing costs:** If premium typefaces are recommended, licensing fees are the client's responsibility (free/open-source alternatives will be provided as fallback options)
- **Print production:** Digital files only; no print coordination or production management

### 2.3 Assumptions

- PayBridge co-founders and 4 key team members are available for interviews during Week 1 (6 interviews, 45 minutes each, conducted remotely)
- PayBridge will provide access to 4 existing customers for 30-minute interviews during Week 1-2
- PayBridge's designated decision-maker (CEO or CMO) can approve deliverables without requiring board-level review
- PayBridge will provide existing materials: current logo files, any marketing collateral, pitch deck, product screenshots, customer testimonials, and competitive intelligence
- Feedback on deliverables will be provided as a single consolidated document per review round, not as rolling comments
- No external agency or additional design contractor is involved in parallel brand work that might conflict with this engagement
- The PayBridge product name and company name are final and will not change during the engagement
