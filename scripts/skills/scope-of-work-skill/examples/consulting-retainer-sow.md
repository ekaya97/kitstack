# Example: Consulting Retainer SOW

## User Input

> Write a SOW for a fractional CTO retainer. Client is MediTrack, a health-tech startup with 8 engineers. They need senior technical leadership -- architecture decisions, hiring help, vendor evaluations, sprint planning, and dev process improvement. 20 hours/month, EUR 180/hr, 6-month minimum. I'm an independent consultant.

## 1. Engagement Overview

MediTrack GmbH requires senior technical leadership to guide architecture decisions, strengthen engineering processes, support hiring, and provide strategic technology oversight as the company scales its engineering team and product. This engagement provides a Fractional CTO operating at 20 hours per month -- sufficient to participate in key decisions, mentor the engineering team, and drive technical strategy without the overhead of a full-time executive hire.

The Fractional CTO will function as a member of the MediTrack leadership team for the duration of the engagement, attending relevant meetings, having direct access to the engineering team, and carrying decision-making authority as delegated by the CEO.

---

## 2. Scope Definition

### 2.1 In Scope

**Technical Strategy & Architecture**
1. Participate in and guide architecture decisions for new features, infrastructure changes, and system design
2. Review and approve technical design documents and architecture proposals from the engineering team
3. Conduct quarterly technology roadmap planning aligned with product and business goals
4. Evaluate build-vs-buy decisions for infrastructure and tooling
5. Assess and manage technical debt -- prioritize remediation within sprint capacity

**Engineering Process & Team**
6. Attend and guide weekly sprint planning sessions (1 hour/week)
7. Review and improve engineering processes: code review practices, CI/CD pipelines, testing strategy, deployment procedures, incident response
8. Conduct bi-weekly 1:1 sessions with engineering lead(s) for mentorship and alignment
9. Establish and track engineering KPIs: deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, sprint velocity, defect rates

**Hiring & Team Development**
10. Define technical roles and write job descriptions for engineering hires
11. Review CVs and conduct technical interviews (up to 4 interviews per month)
12. Design or refine the technical interview process (take-home, system design, cultural fit stages)
13. Onboarding support for new engineering hires (first-week architecture walkthrough)

**Vendor & Technology Evaluation**
14. Evaluate third-party tools, platforms, and services as needed (up to 2 evaluations per month)
15. Manage relationships with key technology vendors (cloud provider, monitoring, CI/CD, etc.)
16. Review and negotiate technical aspects of vendor contracts (not commercial/legal terms)

**Security & Compliance Oversight**
17. Quarterly security posture review: access controls, dependency vulnerabilities, data handling practices
18. Guidance on health-tech-specific compliance considerations (data protection, GDPR, medical device regulations awareness)
19. Incident response planning and post-mortem facilitation

**Leadership & Communication**
20. Attend weekly leadership team meeting (1 hour/week)
21. Provide monthly written technology status report to CEO (progress, risks, recommendations)
22. Represent technical perspective in board updates or investor conversations as needed (up to 1 per quarter)

### 2.2 Out of Scope

- **Hands-on coding or development:** The Fractional CTO provides oversight, review, and guidance -- not production code. Emergency hotfixes are handled by the engineering team with CTO guidance.
- **Full-time availability:** This engagement is 20 hours per month (~5 hours per week). Availability is during agreed working hours, not on-call.
- **HR and people management:** Performance reviews, compensation decisions, and HR processes remain with MediTrack management. CTO provides input on technical performance only.
- **Legal or commercial contract negotiation:** CTO reviews technical aspects of vendor agreements but does not negotiate commercial terms, pricing, or legal clauses.
- **Regulatory certification:** Awareness and guidance on compliance considerations are included; formal certification processes (ISO 27001, SOC 2, MDR) require dedicated compliance resources.
- **Fundraising support beyond technical diligence:** Investor deck review for technical accuracy is included (1x per quarter). Fundraising strategy, investor outreach, and pitch coaching are not.
- **Project management:** Sprint planning participation is included; day-to-day project management, ticket creation, and backlog grooming are the engineering lead's responsibility.

### 2.3 Assumptions

- MediTrack will designate the CEO as the primary point of contact and escalation path for the Fractional CTO
- The Fractional CTO will have direct communication access to all engineering team members via Slack and the project management tool (Jira, Linear, or equivalent)
- The Fractional CTO will have read access to the codebase, CI/CD pipelines, monitoring dashboards, and cloud infrastructure console
- MediTrack's existing engineering lead will continue to handle day-to-day team management, code reviews, and sprint execution
- Meetings will be conducted remotely unless on-site presence is specifically requested (billed separately)
- The 20-hour monthly allocation is an average; individual months may vary by +/- 5 hours based on project needs (overages require written approval)

## 4. Acceptance & Review

**Monthly review:** At the end of each month, the Fractional CTO delivers a written technology status report summarizing activities performed, decisions made, risks identified, and recommendations. The CEO reviews and provides feedback within 5 business days.

**Quarterly business review:** Every 3 months, a 60-minute QBR covers engagement effectiveness, scope adjustments, and fee review. Both parties may propose changes to the monthly scope, hour allocation, or fee.

**Performance metrics tracked:**
- Hours utilized vs. allocated
- Architecture decisions documented and implemented
- Engineering process improvements (measurable: deployment frequency, incident response time)
- Hiring pipeline support (roles defined, interviews conducted, hires made)
- Team satisfaction (qualitative, gathered during QBR)

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

### Service Provider (Fractional CTO)

- Operates as a trusted member of the MediTrack leadership team
- Available during agreed working hours (default: Mon-Fri, 9:00-18:00 CET)
- Responds to Slack/email within 4 business hours during working hours
- Provides written documentation for all significant architecture decisions
- Maintains confidentiality of all MediTrack technical and business information
- Flags capacity conflicts proactively -- if a month's workload will exceed the allocation, notifies the CEO before overages occur

### Client (MediTrack GmbH)

- **Designated contact:** CEO, with authority to approve scope adjustments and Change Orders
- Grants access to codebase, infrastructure, project management tools, and monitoring within 5 business days of engagement start
- Includes Fractional CTO in relevant Slack channels, calendar invitations, and documentation access
- Provides context on business priorities, product roadmap, and company strategy
- Responds to CTO recommendations and questions within 3 business days
- Respects the 20-hour monthly boundary -- does not expect full-time availability or same-day turnaround on non-urgent matters

| Item | Detail | Monthly Investment |

### Payment Terms

- Invoiced monthly in advance, on the 1st of each month
- Payment due within 15 business days of invoice
- Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month

### Hour Management

- **Unused hours:** Do not roll over to the following month
- **Overage hours:** Billed at EUR 180/hr with prior written approval from CEO. Overages exceeding 5 hours in a single month require a Change Order.
- **Hour tracking:** Service Provider maintains a monthly time log by activity category, shared with the CEO in the monthly status report

### Not Included

- Travel expenses for on-site visits (billed at cost + EUR 500 day rate for on-site days; requires 5 business days advance notice)
- Software tools or licenses required for CTO activities (provided by MediTrack)
- External training, certifications, or conference attendance

## 7. Term & Renewal

- **Minimum term:** 6 months from the Effective Date
- **Renewal:** After the minimum term, the engagement continues month-to-month unless terminated
- **Termination during minimum term:** Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. The Client remains responsible for fees through the end of the minimum term or the notice period, whichever is later. This provision does not apply to termination for cause.
- **Termination after minimum term:** Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. No penalty.
- **Fee adjustment:** Either party may propose a fee adjustment at any quarterly business review. Adjusted fees take effect at the start of the following month, with mutual written agreement.
- **Transition support:** Upon termination, the Fractional CTO will provide a transition document (architecture overview, open decisions, in-progress initiatives, recommended next steps) and up to 5 hours of handoff support to a successor, included in the final month's allocation.

---

## 8. Change Order Process

Changes to the monthly scope, hour allocation, fee, or engagement terms require a written Change Order.

**Common Change Order scenarios:**
- Increasing monthly hours (e.g., 20 -> 30 hours for a critical period)
- Adding specific project deliverables (e.g., lead a specific architecture redesign)
- Temporary on-site presence requirement
- Adding team members to the engagement

**Process:**
1. Either party raises a Change Request via email
2. Service Provider responds within 3 business days with proposal
3. CEO approves or rejects within 3 business days
4. Changed terms take effect on the 1st of the following month (or immediately for urgent changes with mutual agreement)

## 9. Terms

- **Intellectual property:** All work product (documents, architecture designs, process frameworks, templates) created during the engagement is the property of MediTrack GmbH. The Service Provider retains the right to use general knowledge, methodologies, and non-confidential frameworks developed before or independently of this engagement.
- **Confidentiality:** The Service Provider will treat all MediTrack technical, business, financial, and product information as strictly confidential. This includes source code, architecture, customer data, hiring plans, and strategic direction. Confidentiality obligations survive for 2 years after engagement termination. A mutual NDA will be executed separately if required.
- **Conflicts of interest:** The Service Provider will not engage with direct competitors of MediTrack in the DACH health-tech space during the engagement term. Other consulting engagements are permitted provided they do not create a conflict of interest or impact availability.
- **Liability:** Service Provider's total liability is capped at 3 months of fees (EUR 10,800). Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.
- **Termination for cause:** Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this SOW and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice.
- **Governing law:** Laws of the Federal Republic of Germany. Venue: Berlin.
