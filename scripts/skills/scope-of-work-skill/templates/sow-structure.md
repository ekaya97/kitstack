<!-- Standard change process. Adapt the timeline for engagement size. -->

**Process:**
1. Either party submits a written Change Request via email
2. Service Provider responds within [3-5] business days with a Change Order proposal (scope, cost, timeline impact)
3. Client approves or rejects within [3-5] business days
4. No work on the change begins until the Change Order is signed
