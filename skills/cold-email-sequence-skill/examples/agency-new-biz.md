# Example: Agency New Business — 5-Email Sequence

## User Input

> I run a performance marketing agency (paid social + paid search). Our sweet spot is DTC e-commerce brands doing $2M-$20M revenue that are scaling ad spend but the founder is still managing ads themselves or working with a freelancer who can't keep up. I want to reach out to DTC founders/marketing leads. Goal: book a strategy call. We have some great case studies.

## Email 2: The Value Add
**Send:** Day 3 (Thursday, 9:00 AM)
**Subject:** 3 quick wins for [company]

Hi [first_name],

Looked at your Meta ads and landing pages a bit more closely. Three things I'd test if I were running [company]'s account:

1. Your bestseller product page loads in 4.2s on mobile — getting that under 2.5s typically improves ROAS by 15-25% alone (GTmetrix is free to check this)

2. Your current ad creative is heavy on product shots. DTC brands at your stage usually see a 30-40% improvement in CTR by adding founder-story or "why we made this" angle creative into the mix

3. You're running broad targeting on most campaigns. At your spend level, a stacked Advantage+ structure with creative-driven audience segmentation usually outperforms manual interest targeting

No pitch here — just observations from running ads for [X] DTC brands. Happy to elaborate on any of these.

[Your name]

## Email 3: The Case Study
**Send:** Day 8 (Tuesday, 9:00 AM)
**Subject:** [similar brand]'s results: [specific metric]

Hi [first_name],

Quick example that might be relevant to [company].

Last year we started working with [Similar Brand] — DTC [category], similar revenue range to yours. They were spending $[X]K/month on Meta and Google with decent but flat results. The founder was managing everything in-house and knew they were leaving money on the table.

In 90 days:
- ROAS improved from 2.8x to 4.6x
- Monthly revenue from paid channels grew from $[X] to $[Y]
- Ad spend increased (profitably) by 65%
- Founder went from 12 hours/week on ads to a 30-minute weekly review call

The biggest lever wasn't some magic strategy — it was structured creative testing. We went from 2-3 new creatives per month to 15-20, with a system for identifying winners within 48 hours.

Worth a 30-minute strategy call to see if a similar approach could work for [company]? I can walk through the specifics of what we'd test first.

## Email 4: The Different Angle
**Send:** Day 13 (Monday, 9:00 AM)
**Subject:** the founder ad spend trap

Hi [first_name],

One more thought for you — this isn't about us, it's about a pattern I see with every DTC founder who manages their own ads:

Your time has a dollar value. If [company] is doing $[X]M, your effective hourly rate as founder is probably $200-500/hour. Every hour you spend adjusting bids, reviewing placements, and resizing creative is an hour you're not spending on product development, partnerships, or strategy.

The math: 10 hours/week on ads x 52 weeks x $300/hour = $156,000/year of founder time spent on a task that a specialized team does better.

Not saying you need us specifically — but the ROI on offloading paid media at your stage almost always pencils out, regardless of who you work with.

Just a thought worth running the numbers on.

[Your name]

## Personalization Notes

- **[company]** — Prospect's brand name
- **[first_name]** — First name
- **Email 1, line 1:** Go to Facebook Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library), search their brand, and reference a specific ad you saw. Mention the creative approach or angle, not just "your ads." This proves you actually looked.
- **Email 2, point 1:** Run their landing page through GTmetrix or PageSpeed Insights. Use the actual load time in your email. If it's already fast, replace with a different observation.
- **Email 2, point 2:** Look at their actual ad creative in the Meta Ad Library. Adjust the observation to match what they're actually running.
- **Email 3:** Replace [Similar Brand] with a REAL case study. Ideally in the same or adjacent product category. Use real numbers.
- **Email 4:** Estimate their revenue from public signals (press, LinkedIn, SimilarWeb) to make the hourly rate calculation feel specific, not generic.

## A/B Testing Suggestions

**Email 1 subject line variants:**
- A: `[company]'s paid social` (direct, company-specific)
- B: `noticed something in [company]'s ad library` (curiosity-driven, shows you did research)

**Email 3 subject line variants:**
- A: `[similar brand]'s results: 2.8x → 4.6x ROAS` (result-driven, specific numbers)
- B: `what happened when [similar brand] stopped managing ads in-house` (story-driven)

## Sequence Notes

This 5-email sequence is appropriate because:
1. The ask (strategy call) requires more trust than "try a free tool"
2. Agency services are high-ticket, so the prospect needs multiple touchpoints
3. Email 2 provides genuine, specific value that demonstrates expertise
4. Email 4 introduces a completely different angle (time cost vs. ad performance) for prospects who didn't respond to the performance angle
5. The breakup email includes a low-friction offer (free audit) that often converts prospects who were interested but not ready
