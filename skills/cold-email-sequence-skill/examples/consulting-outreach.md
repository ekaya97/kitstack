# Example: Consulting Outreach — 4-Email Sequence

## User Input

> I'm a fractional COO helping B2B SaaS companies (Series A-B) fix their operations — messy handoffs between sales and CS, no clear metrics, founders doing everything. I want to reach out to SaaS founders who recently raised Series A. Goal: book a 20-minute intro call.

---

## Generated Sequence

# Cold Email Sequence: Fractional COO → Series A SaaS Founders

**Target:** Founders/CEOs of B2B SaaS companies that raised Series A in the last 6 months, 15-50 employees
**Goal:** Book a 20-minute intro call
**Sequence:** 4 emails over 14 days

---

## Email 1: The Opener
**Send:** Day 0 (Tuesday or Wednesday, 8:30 AM recipient's timezone)
**Subject:** [company]'s post-series A ops

Hi [first_name],

Congrats on [company]'s Series A — [investor name] is a strong partner for what comes next.

The thing I see with most Series A companies around your stage: the team that got you here (generalists, founder-led decisions, duct-tape processes) starts to break around 25-30 people. Sales closes a deal, CS doesn't know about it until the customer complains. Metrics exist in 4 different spreadsheets.

I'm a fractional COO who helps B2B SaaS companies build the operational layer between "scrappy startup" and "real company" — without the overhead of a full-time hire.

Is ops something you're actively thinking about, or is it still manageable?

[Your name]

---

## Email 2: The Value Add
**Send:** Day 3 (Friday, 9:00 AM)
**Subject:** quick thought on [company]

Hi [first_name],

One thing I've noticed with post-Series A SaaS companies: the first operational hire founders consider is usually a VP of Sales or VP of CS. But the bottleneck is rarely in one team — it's in the handoffs between them.

The cheapest operational fix I know: a shared "customer lifecycle" doc that maps exactly what happens from signed deal → onboarding → first value milestone → renewal. Takes 2 hours to build, saves 10+ hours per week of ad-hoc Slack questions.

Happy to share the template I use if it's useful — no strings attached.

[Your name]

---

## Email 3: The Social Proof
**Send:** Day 7 (Tuesday, 8:30 AM)
**Subject:** how [similar company] fixed their handoff problem

Hi [first_name],

Last year I worked with [Similar Company] — Series A SaaS, about your size. Their founder was spending 15 hours/week on internal coordination: sitting in every sales-to-CS handoff, resolving process disputes, manually updating dashboards.

We built three things in 6 weeks: a standardized handoff process, automated lifecycle tracking in their existing CRM, and a single weekly ops dashboard that replaced 4 spreadsheets.

Result: founder got 15 hours/week back. CS onboarding time dropped from 21 days to 9. Net revenue retention went from 94% to 108% within two quarters.

Worth a 20-minute call to see if something similar applies to [company]?

[Your name]

---

## Email 4: The Breakup
**Send:** Day 14 (Tuesday, 8:30 AM)
**Subject:** closing the loop

Hi [first_name],

I've reached out a few times about operational support for [company] — I'll keep this one short.

If ops isn't a pain point right now, totally fair. If it's more of a "not yet but eventually" situation, happy to reconnect in a few months.

Either way, I'll stop filling your inbox. Just say the word if you'd like to chat down the line.

[Your name]

---

## Personalization Notes

- **[company]** — Replace with prospect's company name
- **[first_name]** — Prospect's first name
- **[investor name]** — Lead investor from their Series A (find on Crunchbase or press release)
- **[Similar Company]** — Replace with a real client you've worked with, ideally in a similar space
- **Email 1, paragraph 2:** Adjust the specific operational challenge based on what you know about the prospect's company (hiring signals, Glassdoor reviews, LinkedIn posts from employees can reveal pain points)

## A/B Testing Suggestions

**Email 1 subject line variants:**
- A: `[company]'s post-series A ops` (specific, company-focused)
- B: `the 25-person wall` (curiosity-driven, speaks to a known pain point)

**Email 3 CTA variants:**
- A: "Worth a 20-minute call?" (specific time ask)
- B: "Curious if this pattern looks familiar?" (interest check, lower friction)
