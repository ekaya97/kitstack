# Acceptable Ranges

Normal ranges for key contract terms across different engagement types. Use these benchmarks to determine risk levels during contract scanning.

---

## Payment Terms

| Engagement Type | Standard (GREEN) | Unfavorable (YELLOW) | Dangerous (RED) |
|----------------|-------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Freelance / Small Project | Net 14 | Net 30 | Net 45+ |
| Agency / Mid-size Project | Net 30 | Net 45 | Net 60+ |
| Enterprise / Large Project | Net 30-45 | Net 60 | Net 90+ |
| Retainer / Ongoing | Net 14 (monthly) | Net 30 | Net 45+ |

**Notes:**
- EU Late Payment Directive (2011/7/EU) sets 30 days as the default for B2B. Exceeding 60 days is generally considered unreasonable.
- In Germany, SS 271 BGB makes payment due immediately unless otherwise agreed. SS 286 BGB triggers default (Verzug) after 30 days from invoice receipt.
- Upfront payment percentage: 30-50% upfront is standard for project work. 0% upfront with Net 60+ is a major cash flow risk.

## Upfront Payment

| Project Size | Standard (GREEN) | Low (YELLOW) | Risky (RED) |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------|
| Under EUR 5,000 | 50% upfront | 30% upfront | 0% upfront |
| EUR 5,000-20,000 | 33-50% upfront | 20% upfront | 0-10% upfront |
| EUR 20,000-100,000 | 25-33% upfront | 15% upfront | 0% upfront |
| EUR 100,000+ | 20-25% upfront | 10% upfront | 0% upfront |

## Liability Caps

| Metric | Standard (GREEN) | Elevated (YELLOW) | Dangerous (RED) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Cap relative to fees | 1x total fees paid | 2-3x total fees paid | 5x+ or unlimited |
| Absolute cap (small project) | EUR 10,000-50,000 | EUR 100,000 | EUR 500,000+ or unlimited |
| Absolute cap (enterprise) | EUR 100,000-500,000 | EUR 1M-5M | EUR 10M+ or unlimited |
| Consequential damages | Excluded | Limited | Included without cap |

**Notes:**
- Liability caps cannot exclude liability for intent (Vorsatz) or gross negligence (grobe Fahrlassigkeit) under German law.
- For freelancers, liability exceeding 1x the contract value is disproportionate.
- Professional indemnity insurance (Berufshaftpflicht) typically covers EUR 250,000-1,000,000 — your liability cap should not exceed your insurance coverage.

## Non-Compete / Non-Solicitation

| Term | Standard (GREEN) | Unfavorable (YELLOW) | Dangerous (RED) |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Duration | 6 months post-term | 12 months | 18+ months |
| Geographic scope | City/region | Country | Global |
| Industry scope | Named competitors (3-5) | "Direct competitors" (undefined) | "Any competing business" |
| Compensation (Germany) | 50%+ of last compensation | Less than 50% | No compensation |
| Compensation (US) | Varies by state | — | Required in some states |

**Notes:**
- Germany: Post-contractual non-competes without Karenzentschadigung (compensation) are generally unenforceable for employees (SS 74 HGB). For freelancers, the same principles typically apply by analogy.
- California: Non-competes are void (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code SS 16600).
- UK: Must be "reasonable" in scope and duration. Courts regularly strike down broad non-competes.
- EU: Proposed directive may standardize non-compete protections across member states.

## Termination Notice Periods

| Engagement Type | Standard (GREEN) | Short (YELLOW) | Dangerous (RED) |
|----------------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Project (< 3 months) | 14 days | 7 days | Immediate / 0 days |
| Project (3-6 months) | 30 days | 14 days | 7 days or less |
| Retainer / Ongoing | 30 days | 14 days | 7 days or less |
| Employment (Germany) | Per SS 622 BGB (4 weeks-7 months based on tenure) | Below statutory minimum | — |

**Kill fee benchmarks:**
- Standard: Payment for work completed + 25% of remaining contract value
- Favorable: Payment for work completed + 50% of remaining contract value
- Unfavorable: Payment for work completed only
- Dangerous: No payment for work-in-progress

## Revision Rounds

| Engagement Type | Standard (GREEN) | High (YELLOW) | Dangerous (RED) |
|----------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Design / Branding | 2-3 rounds | 4-5 rounds | Unlimited |
| Writing / Content | 2 rounds | 3-4 rounds | Unlimited |
| Development | Testing + 1 revision cycle | Multiple QA cycles | "Until satisfactory" |
| Strategy / Consulting | 1 round on draft | 2-3 rounds | Unlimited |

**Notes:**
- Each "round" should be defined: "A round consists of a single consolidated set of feedback. Client collects all stakeholder input and delivers it as one document."
- Additional rounds should be billed: EUR 75-200/hour depending on service type.

## Confidentiality Duration

| Type | Standard (GREEN) | Long (YELLOW) | Excessive (RED) |
|------|-------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| General business info | 2-3 years post-term | 5 years | Indefinite |
| Trade secrets | 5 years or until publicly available | 10 years | Indefinite |
| Personal data (GDPR) | Governed by GDPR obligations | — | — |

**Notes:**
- Indefinite confidentiality obligations are common in NDAs but should be limited to genuine trade secrets.
- Carve-outs must include: information already public, independently developed, received from third parties, required by law.

## Warranty Periods

| Deliverable Type | Standard (GREEN) | Long (YELLOW) | Excessive (RED) |
|-----------------|-------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Software/Development | 30-90 days post-delivery | 6 months | 12+ months |
| Design/Creative | 14-30 days post-delivery | 60 days | 6+ months |
| Strategy/Consulting | None (advisory only) | 30 days | Any performance warranty |
| Physical products (Germany) | 24 months (SS 438 BGB) | — | Below statutory minimum |

**Notes:**
- Warranty should cover defects in the deliverables against the agreed specification, not business outcomes.
- Bug fixes during warranty are at no cost. Feature requests are new scope.
- In Germany, the statutory warranty period for Werkvertrag (work contracts) is 24 months (SS 634a BGB). This cannot be shortened in AGB for consumers.

## Insurance Requirements

| Project Size | Standard (GREEN) | High (YELLOW) | Excessive (RED) |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Under EUR 10,000 | No requirement | EUR 500K coverage | EUR 1M+ coverage |
| EUR 10,000-50,000 | EUR 250K-500K | EUR 1M | EUR 5M+ |
| EUR 50,000-200,000 | EUR 500K-1M | EUR 2M | EUR 5M+ |
| EUR 200,000+ | EUR 1M-2M | EUR 5M | EUR 10M+ |

**Common insurance types requested:**
- Professional indemnity (Berufshaftpflicht) — standard and reasonable
- General liability (Betriebshaftpflicht) — standard
- Cyber liability — increasingly common for tech projects
- Errors & omissions — standard for consulting
