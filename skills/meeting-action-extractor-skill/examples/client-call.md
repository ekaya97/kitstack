> Call with Petra from GreenTech Solutions - March 18, 30 mins
> 
> Petra called to discuss the website redesign project we proposed last month. She's ready to move forward but has some concerns.
>
> Budget: The EUR 24,000 we quoted is approved, but their CFO wants to split payments differently. Instead of our standard thirds (signing, midpoint, delivery), they want 20% on signing, 40% at midpoint, 40% on delivery. I said I'd check internally and get back to her.
>
> Timeline: They need the site live by June 1st for a trade fair. That's 10 weeks from now which is tight. I think it's doable if they're responsive on feedback. Petra said she can commit to 48-hour turnaround on reviews but needs to check with her colleague Jan who handles the product content.
>
> Content: Speaking of Jan, he's apparently the bottleneck. He was supposed to deliver product descriptions and photos by now but hasn't. Petra apologized and said she'd push him. I told her the content needs to be ready by April 8 at the latest or the June 1 deadline is at risk. She understood.
>
> Scope change: They now want to add a career page with job listings. Wasn't in the original scope. I said we can add it but it will affect either budget or timeline. She asked me to quote the addition separately.
>
> I mentioned our requirement for a Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV) since we'll be handling their customer data (contact forms, newsletter signups). She said their legal team will prepare one. I need to send her our standard AVV template as a starting point.
>
> She also asked if we could do the site in both German and English. I said multilingual adds significant complexity and wasn't in scope. We could do German-first and plan English as a phase 2. She seemed okay with that.
>
> We agreed to have the kick-off call next week once the contract is signed. I'll send the updated contract with the new payment terms today or tomorrow. Petra will get it signed by EOW.
>
> Oh also she mentioned they might need social media templates that match the new brand. I said we could discuss that after the website is done - not related to this project.
