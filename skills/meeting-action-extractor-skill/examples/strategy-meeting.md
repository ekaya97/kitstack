# Example: Strategy Meeting — Before/After

## Raw Meeting Notes (Input)

The user pastes the following unstructured notes:

---

> Strategy meeting 3/14 - Q2 planning
>
> Present: Sarah (CEO), Marcus (Head of Product), Tom (Engineering Lead), Lisa (Marketing)
>
> Started talking about Q2 priorities. Sarah wants to focus on enterprise. Marcus thinks we should fix the onboarding first because churn is at 8% and most of it happens in the first 14 days. Tom agrees with Marcus - says the onboarding flow has technical debt that causes bugs.
>
> Debate about enterprise vs. fixing onboarding. Sarah eventually agrees - let's fix onboarding first (April), then start enterprise push in May.
>
> Marcus will put together a redesigned onboarding flow mockup by end of next week. Needs Tom's input on what's technically feasible. Tom said he'll audit the current onboarding code and identify the worst bugs this week.
>
> Lisa asked about the Q2 marketing budget. Sarah said she'll get approval from the board by March 25. Lisa needs the budget before she can finalize the Q2 campaign plan. She mentioned she's been talking to an agency about paid social but hasn't decided yet.
>
> Sarah brought up that we need a better CRM. Currently using spreadsheets. Marcus suggested HubSpot. Tom said he looked at HubSpot before and it was expensive. Sarah said let's table that for now and revisit when we have the budget clarity.
>
> Tom mentioned we need to hire another engineer. The team is stretched thin. Sarah agreed but said hiring is paused until after the board meeting. Tom should write the job description anyway so we're ready to post when hiring opens.
>
> Marcus asked about the customer advisory board idea from last month. Nobody has followed up. Sarah said it's still a good idea but let's focus on Q2 first.
>
> Quick note - the office lease renewal is coming up in May. Sarah will handle.
>
> Next meeting: March 21, same time.

---

## Processed Output

# Meeting Summary: Q2 Strategy Planning

**Date:** March 14
**Attendees:** Sarah (CEO), Marcus (Head of Product), Tom (Engineering Lead), Lisa (Marketing)
**Next meeting:** March 21

---

## Decisions Made

1. **Q2 priority sequence:** Fix onboarding first (April), then begin enterprise push (May). Onboarding churn at 8% (concentrated in first 14 days) was the deciding factor.
2. **CRM evaluation deferred:** Tabled the CRM discussion (HubSpot vs. alternatives) until budget clarity after board meeting.
3. **Customer advisory board deferred:** Good idea but deprioritized for Q2. Revisit after Q2 priorities are underway.
4. **Hiring paused:** No new hires posted until after board meeting, but preparation can proceed.

## Key Discussion Points

The core debate was whether to prioritize enterprise expansion or fix the onboarding experience. The 8% churn rate concentrated in the first 14 days was the decisive data point — acquiring enterprise customers while losing existing customers at that rate was deemed counterproductive. The team aligned on a sequential approach: fix onboarding in April, then pivot to enterprise in May.
